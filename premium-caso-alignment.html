Most persuasive interview narrative.

The evidence is strongest when presented as one operating model rather than a collection of projects: accreditation creates the standards; institutional effectiveness creates the evidence; strategic planning defines the direction; CQI closes the loop; data governance makes the evidence trustworthy; academic health-system partnerships make the strategy executable; and AI/digital governance extends the same discipline into emerging technology.

Explicit gaps to manage, not hide.

Formal line supervision

KPSOM asks for eight years of team supervision. The record supports extensive matrix leadership, but it should not be represented as equivalent direct-report supervision.

WSCUC / ALO

SACSCOC data-response experience is credible institutional-accreditation transfer evidence, but the portfolio should never claim WSCUC or ALO experience.

BPPE

No direct BPPE experience is documented. The application should emphasize rapid standards translation and broad accreditation/regulatory fluency rather than blur this distinction.
