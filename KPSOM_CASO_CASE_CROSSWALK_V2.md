## Explicit qualification boundaries
- No WSCUC Accreditation Liaison Officer experience is claimed.
- No BPPE experience is claimed.
- No eight-year direct-report supervision record is claimed.
- Matrix leadership, governance, facilitation, project-team coordination and executive advising are presented as such and are not relabeled as direct staff supervision.
- SACSCOC work is presented as institutional accreditation data-response support, not as service as the formal accreditation liaison.
- Successful LCME/ACGME/ACCME outcomes are framed as major institutional contribution, not sole ownership.

## Public-page strategy
The public portfolio should not say “Kaiser fit.” Instead, the first ten cases are ordered so the CASO operating mandate is visible without requiring the reader to translate from unrelated project categories. Cases 11–18 demonstrate the breadth that differentiates the candidate beyond the minimum role specification.
