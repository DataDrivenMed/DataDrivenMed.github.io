# Development of an Evidence-Based Model for Academic and Community Health Center Collaboration (Chauvin S, Paragi R)

## Strategic Execution and Model Design
I collaborated to develop an evidence-based, systematic model designed to significantly enhance collaborations between academic institutions and community health centers. My work specifically targeted the implementation and execution of academic residency and clinical rotations within Louisiana's Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs). Through this initiative, I helped establish a highly standardized, reproducible framework for integrating educational rotations into community health center settings. 

## Technical Execution and Framework Application
I ensured that the model was crafted to be highly adaptable and easily applied by both academic and community health center leaders nationwide. The technical structure of the model serves as a practical blueprint for establishing new, effective clinical rotations and community-based learning experiences. A key technical feature of this framework is the incorporation of inter-professional learning teams, which allows institutions to enhance and expand their existing educational programs while delivering high-quality training.

## Impact and Outcomes
This evidence-based model provides a standardized, scalable approach to experiential education that can be utilized by academic and community health center leaders across the United States. By bridging the gap between academic institutions and FQHCs, my work ensures the sustainable growth of community-based learning experiences and strengthens the collaborative infrastructure necessary to train future healthcare professionals in diverse practice settings.
