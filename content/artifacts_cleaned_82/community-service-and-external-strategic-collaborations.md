# Community Service Activities and External Collaborations

## Strategic Execution and Cross-Institutional Partnerships
I actively drive high-level strategic partnerships across academic, community, and international healthcare institutions to advance medical education and clinical training. From 2009 to 2012, I facilitated an ongoing collaboration between LSUHSC-New Orleans faculty and community leaders at Excelth, Inc. and Jefferson Parish Community Health Centers (FQHCs). Serving as the primary liaison between academic and community leadership, I successfully established a new partnership and created a realistic, standardized model for an inter-professional academic rotation involving Family Medicine residents and Nurse Practitioner students. 

Expanding my collaborative scope internationally, I partnered with the Centre for Patient Safety and Service Quality (CPSSQ) at Imperial College in London and the Gippsland Medical School at Monash University in Australia from 2009 to 2010. Through this global initiative, I developed highly effective debriefing techniques optimized for experiential learning in operative rooms utilizing advanced simulation-based exercises. Concurrently, through the American Surgical Education Research Fellowship (SERF), I collaborated directly with a clinical fellow from Imperial College London to develop and refine a simulation-based teamwork training debriefing assessment tool, which I originally co-developed with my internal academic colleagues. Furthermore, in 2017, I provided expert consultation to the Graduate Medical Education (GME) department at the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio (UTHSCSA). I educated key stakeholders on the intricate framework and operational mechanisms of Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) Medicare funding and associated Cost Allocation Plans (CAPs), significantly enhancing their ability to navigate these complex financial systems effectively.

## Technical Execution and Data Analytics
I apply rigorous data management and complex analytics to support systemic public health initiatives, workforce planning, and educational equity. From 2007 to 2016, I led the comprehensive evaluation and data management for the Southeast Louisiana Area Health Education Center and the Louisiana Student/Resident Experiences and Rotations in Community Health (LA-SEARCH) program. From 2010 to 2020, I provided extensive technical consultation to the Louisiana Department of Health & Hospitals (DHH), specifically collaborating with the Bureau of Primary Care and Rural Health’s Recruitment and Retention Services Unit. In this capacity, I conducted sophisticated manpower studies to accurately designate Louisiana Health Professional Shortage Areas (HPSA) and Medically Underserved Areas (MUA). 

I also delivered critical data support for an equity and performance study in collaboration with the Tulane School of Medicine. I assisted the Assistant Dean for Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion by gathering, organizing, and structuring highly specific data for a quantitative study designed to examine the predictive ability of MCAT scores on the pre-clinical grade point averages of Black medical students. Additionally, as a member of the Louisiana State Medical Education Commission (established by the state legislature) from 2010 to 2019, I managed an internet interface utilized to share comprehensive GME data, programmatic descriptions, and emerging medical education trends both nationally and across Louisiana.

## Impact and Outcomes
My data-driven collaborations and community service activities have yielded tangible improvements in statewide healthcare workforce distribution and the quality of international medical training. By systematically evaluating and managing data for the LA-SEARCH program, I directly improved the overarching supply and distribution of healthcare professionals across the state by strengthening community and academic educational partnerships. My technical consulting and manpower studies for the DHH directly enabled the state to accurately designate critical shortage areas, paving the way for targeted recruitment and resource allocation for vulnerable populations. 

Through my sustained, nine-year involvement with the Louisiana State Medical Education Commission, I engaged in an annual cycle of meetings, public reports, and issue discussions, ensuring that critical GME data continually informed legislative healthcare policy and institutional planning. Finally, my international collaborations in simulation-based assessment successfully refined teamwork training methodologies, establishing standardized debriefing tools that continue to enhance patient safety and experiential learning in operating rooms.
