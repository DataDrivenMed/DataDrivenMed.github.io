## Original Source Content

I initiated and led LSU Health New Orleans's response to FDA's Request for Information on AI-enabled real-time clinical trials (Docket No. FDA-2026-N-4390). Working from my own reading of the docket, I developed the strategic brief and drafted the full institutional RFI response for review by Dean Richard DiCarlo and Dr. Lucio Miele, Director of the LSU Health-LCMC Cancer Center. The work included designing a Gulf South consortium architecture, analyzing the competitive landscape against NCI-designated centers already selected for proof-of-concept trials, and navigating the institutional politics of a threesystem affiliate environment to arrive at a defensible two-institution founding proposal. The submission positions LSU Health as a Tier 2 pilot participant with a defined pathway to full real-time data streaming, grounded in a rare disease oncology anchor at East Jefferson General Hospital that no peer institution can replicate.
