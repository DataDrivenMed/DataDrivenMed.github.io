## **Professional Experience**

## **Program Lead | AHRQ TeamSTEPPS & Patient Safety Initiative**

*LSU Health Sciences Center, New Orleans*

- **Global First:** Directed the first-ever implementation of a point-of-care (*in situ*) human patient simulation program specifically designed for surgical teamwork training.
- **Innovative Methodology:** Engineered the "System for Teamwork Effectiveness and Patient Safety" (STEPS), utilizing a **Mobile Mock Operating Room (MMOR)** to deliver standardized crisis management training within actual clinical environments.
- **Educational Design:** Developed a proprietary **structured debriefing tool** specifically for the project, enabling teams to translate simulation performance into actionable clinical improvements and sustained behavioral change.
- **Strategic Leadership:** Spearheaded the integration of AHRQ/DoD **TeamSTEPPS™** curriculum with high-fidelity, mixed-reality simulation to enhance communication, leadership, and situational awareness among multidisciplinary surgical teams.
- **Peer-Reviewed Recognition:** Established a world-first training model (cited in 2006) that transformed interprofessional medical education and set the global benchmark for hospital-based patient safety programs.
