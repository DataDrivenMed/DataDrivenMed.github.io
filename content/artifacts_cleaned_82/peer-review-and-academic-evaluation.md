# Peer Review and Academic Evaluation

## Strategic Execution and Scholarly Review
I actively contribute to the rigor and advancement of medical literature by serving as a dedicated manuscript reviewer for several prestigious, peer-reviewed publications. Since 2020, I have evaluated complex scholarly submissions for the *Journal of General Internal Medicine*. Additionally, I have served as an ongoing manuscript reviewer for the *Annals of Family Medicine* since 2019 and for the *British Medical Journals* out of London, U.K., since 2010. My earlier scholarly review work includes analyzing manuscripts for the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ) publication, *Advances in Patient Safety: New Directions and Alternative Approaches*, based in Rockville, MD, in August 2008. Through these sustained roles, I critically assess research methodologies and support the dissemination of high-quality, evidence-based medical knowledge.

## Technical Execution and Program Evaluation
Beyond manuscript review, I apply my analytical expertise to evaluate academic conference abstracts and institutional grant initiatives. I served as an abstract reviewer for the Southern Group on Educational Affairs (SGEA) Annual Conference for two consecutive years. In April 2008, I reviewed academic abstracts centered on the theme "Medical Education: Balancing Learning Strategies And Technologies." Subsequently, in October 2009, I evaluated submissions focused on the theme "Effective Teamwork = Excellence in Patient Care." 

Furthermore, I operated as an Evaluator and Analyst for the National Cancer Institute (NCI) Cancer Education Grant program from 2007 to 2008. In this capacity, I rigorously assessed the "Short Research Experiences in Cancer" program at the Louisiana State University Health Science Center to ensure educational effectiveness and strict alignment with institutional grant objectives.
