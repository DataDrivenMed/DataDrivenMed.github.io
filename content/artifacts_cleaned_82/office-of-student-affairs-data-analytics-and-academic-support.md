# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Academic Support
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, my primary responsibility within the Office of Student Affairs centers on utilizing advanced data analytics to comprehensively inform and enhance student support strategies. I systematically aim to improve student academic performance, optimize career advising, elevate residency match outcomes, and maximize the quality of educational resources across the medical school. I rigorously evaluate the real-world effectiveness of our academic support services, recommending targeted enhancements based on data-driven insights to heavily foster student success. Furthermore, I directly utilize data analytics to inform and shape our institutional career advising strategies. Beyond direct student support, I comprehensively assess the availability, overall utilization, and practical effectiveness of our educational resources and physical infrastructure. I also ensure the highly accurate and efficient management of educational records, leveraging analytics to continuously improve operational processes and maintain strict compliance with overarching educational standards.

## Technical Execution and Performance Analysis
I drive deep technical analysis by examining National Board of Medical Examiners (NBME) and United States Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE) data. By thoroughly analyzing these critical scores, I accurately assess student performance trends and pinpoint specific cohorts or areas requiring dedicated academic support interventions. Additionally, I conduct comprehensive, deep-dive analyses of complex residency match data to proactively identify historical trends, institutional strengths, and strategic areas for improvement. This rigorous analysis yields actionable insights that are crucial for optimizing our overall residency match success rates. To ensure these insights are highly accessible, I generate tailored reports and dynamic dashboards that clearly showcase key metrics related to student performance, residency match outcomes, and educational resource utilization. These data visualization tools directly empower informed, strategic decision-making by school administrators and the faculty.

## Impact and Cross-Functional Collaboration
My analytical framework directly impacts the broader institution by actively promoting a pervasive, data-driven culture within the Office of Student Affairs. I consistently and effectively communicate my complex analytical findings to key stakeholders, ensuring transparency and institutional alignment. To achieve comprehensive support across the board, I collaborate extensively with various cross-functional teams throughout the medical school. Through this continuous collaboration, I ensure total alignment in our overarching student support strategies and widely share the critical insights I glean from my data analysis, ultimately driving a unified, institutional approach to medical student success.
