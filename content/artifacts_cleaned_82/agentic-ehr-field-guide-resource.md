## Summary

The Agentic EHR Field Guide is a live public governance and education artifact focused on agentic AI in EHR-adjacent clinical and operational environments. It frames EHR-connected AI as a high-governance domain where autonomy, permissions, tool access, documentation, patient communication, billing, clinical workflow, and auditability require careful institutional and health-system review.

The artifact is especially relevant for academic health sciences environments that educate learners and conduct research across partner clinical systems. It helps distinguish internal education and planning from partner-governed clinical, EHR, operational, and revenue-cycle implementation.

## Strategic value

This artifact demonstrates the ability to translate agentic AI into a practical field guide for clinical governance, implementation readiness, and risk classification. It supports leadership conversations about what academic health sciences institutions can prepare internally and what requires formal health-system partner approval.

## Evidence boundary

This is a public educational and governance resource. It does not authorize EHR integration, clinical automation, patient communication, documentation generation, billing actions, or operational deployment. Any EHR-connected or clinical workflow requires health-system partner governance, privacy review, security review, clinical validation, and human oversight.
