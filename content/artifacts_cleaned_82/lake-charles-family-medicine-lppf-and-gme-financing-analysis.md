## **Under Professional Experience (LSU Health New Orleans, Director of Strategic Outcomes)**

**Narrative blurb:** Provided policy and financial analysis to support the Lake Charles Family Medicine Residency during a contentious regional dispute over Louisiana's Local Provider Participation Fund (LPPF). Synthesized Medicaid financing mechanics, hospital competitive dynamics, and GME training implications into a single strategic brief that reframed the issue for program leadership and gave them a coherent position to bring to hospital administration.

## **Bullets:**

- Analyzed LPPF participation economics for Lake Charles Memorial Hospital, modeling federal match leverage (~2:1 ratio) and projected multi-million-dollar supplemental payment gaps by 2027 under non-participation scenarios
- Diagnosed the underlying competitive threat driving West Calcasieu Cameron Hospital's opposition — payer mix erosion from commercial-to-Medicaid shift and distinguished legitimate financial concern from political posturing, giving program leadership a cleaner frame for negotiations
- Identified GME-specific stakes of the LPPF dispute, including resident training volume risk in pediatrics and the potential loss of a rural/CAH rotation site, and proposed a dual-hospital engagement strategy using residency rotations as a relationship tool

## **Leadership & Service Section**

**Narrative blurb:** When the Lake Charles Family Medicine Residency program director faced pressure from hospital administration to take a position on a politically charged Medicaid financing dispute, I stepped in with independent analysis. The situation involved competing financial interests between two health systems, a state-level supplemental payment program, and downstream GME training implications — none of which had been clearly disentangled. My brief gave the program director and department chair the grounding to engage hospital leadership from a position of institutional understanding rather than reaction.

## **Bullets:**

- Served as informal policy advisor to GME program leadership during a multistakeholder Medicaid financing dispute (LPPF, Calcasieu Parish, 2025), bridging health policy, hospital finance, and GME strategy in a single analysis
- Distilled complex Medicaid supplemental payment mechanics (CEA, DISH, LPPF) into actionable intelligence for non-finance clinical leadership navigating a politically sensitive institutional decision
- Demonstrated cross-functional collaboration across School of Medicine administration and affiliate GME program leadership without formal authority or direct reporting relationship
