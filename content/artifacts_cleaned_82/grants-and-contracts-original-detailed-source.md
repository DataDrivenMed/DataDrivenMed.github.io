## **Illinois WISEWOMAN Program, CDC's Division for Heart Disease and Stroke Prevention (DHDSP) program.**

Date Awarded: 2002 Role: Data Analyst

Study Type: Interventional Clinical Study

The Illinois WISEWOMAN Program's mission is to provide Illinois Breast and Cervical Cancer eligible participants, who are low-income, under- or uninsured 40-64 year old, with the knowledge, skills, and opportunities to improve diet, physical activity, and other lifestyle behaviors to prevent, delay, and control cardiovascular and other chronic diseases. Following lipid, glucose, and blood pressure measurements, IWP participants can choose one of four lifestyle interventions that best suits their risks and motivations. Unique to the IWP is Level 3 Lifestyle Intervention, which consists of four fun dietary and physical activity sessions designed to empower Illinois WISEWOMAN Program participants to prevent, delay, and control cardiovascular and other chronic diseases.

## **Evaluation of the System for Teamwork Effectiveness and Patient Safety (STEPS), New Orleans, LA**

Date Awarded: 2006/09 Agency: NIH ( AHRQ)

Role: Data Analyst/Project Manager Study Type: Special Emphasis Panel

Implement, evaluate, and refine an interdisciplinary, simulation-based training model designed to improve teamwork and patient safety in OR environments. Known as the System for Teamwork Effectiveness and Patient Safety (STEPS), the approach entails a mobile mock operating room (MMOR) configuration that supports training with actual interdisciplinary teams in their own ORs, and enables development and implementation of change process strategies for enhancing teamwork and patient safety at the point of care. The MMOR-based simulations use realistic OR scenarios, specific team-based strategies, and reflective debriefings guided by video replay. The project assesses the extent to which STEPS, as a comprehensive simulation and change process strategy, impacts teamwork and a culture of patient safety. It also examines how individual and organizational factors influence transfer of training to on- the-job team performance.

## **Louisiana CHC Academic Residency/Rotation Model, Louisiana Primary Care Association.**

Date Awarded: 2009/02

Agency: Louisiana Primary Care Association

Role: Data Analyst/Project Manager

To develop an evidence-based, systematic approach to increasing academic-community health center collaboration/partnerships and academic residency and clinical rotations in community health centers, specifically the Louisiana FQHCs. The focus was to provide a standardized model for academic rotations in the Louisiana's FQHCs and create a model for any academic or CHC leader in the United States could use to create an effective rotation or other type of CHC based learning experience or in improving or expanding an existing rotation, including the introduction of inter professional learning teams and community-based experiences.

### **Louisiana Managed Care Incentive Program**

Date Contracted: 02/2018

Agency: Louisiana Department of Health

Role: Consultant.

The Managed Care Incentive Payment (MCIP) program is a new addition to LDH's Healthy Louisiana Managed Care Organization (MCO) contracts. It leverages federal regulations1 allowing State Medicaid programs to include incentive arrangements in MCO contracts. One of two hospital networks formed to contract with participating MCOs on behalf of participating hospitals (Louisiana Quality Network). LSUHSC-NO functions as network administrator and liaison with the MCOs for LQN. As a consultant, I was instrumental in developing the operations and steering committee along with the informatics framework in collaboration with the LSU Health Sciences Center - New Orleans Vice Chancellor of Finance and members of the Louisiana Department of Health (LDH). My role encompassed active participation in the operations, steering, and informatics committees, ensuring a cohesive and strategic approach to our initiatives.

## **LSUHSC New Orleans Center for Advanced Learning and Simulation CALS Project**

Date Awarded: Pending Funding

Agency: U.S. House Appropriations Labor, Health and Human Services, Education Subcommittee

through US Congressman Troy Carer's Appropriations Community Project Funding.

Role: Evaluation Consultant Funding Requested: \$10 million

The project budget for the LSUHSC New Orleans Center for Advanced Learning and Simulation CALS includes \$500,000.00 capital outlay to fully equip our 16 patient-learner station Standardized Patient Project. Creating an environment that simulates medical offices and patient hospital rooms is critical in preparing learners to interface with patients and their families. The CALS Wet Lab and six simulated operating rooms on the second floor will require \$6.5 million for surgical equipment, critical care beds, OR tables, high fidelity simulators, CAT Scanner, and OR equipment. LSUHSC New Orleans can fulfill any federal matching requirements.
