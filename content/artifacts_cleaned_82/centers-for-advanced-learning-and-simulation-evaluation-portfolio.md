# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Evaluation Frameworks
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I lead comprehensive evaluation initiatives for the LSUHSC New Orleans Center for Advanced Learning and Simulation (CALS) Project. I designed and implemented overarching, highly comprehensive evaluation frameworks targeting multiple complex components of the CALS Project. To accurately capture the full scope of the center's impact, I developed robust qualitative and quantitative assessment tools. These specialized tools were engineered to rigorously measure critical educational outcomes, evaluate tangible improvements in clinical performance, and assess overall operational efficiency across the entire CALS initiative. Furthermore, I actively supported continuous funding opportunities for the center by leveraging my strategic insights to write and develop competitive grant proposals.

## Technical Execution and Longitudinal Analytics
To ensure rigorous, data-driven decision-making within the CALS Project, I led the complex integration of advanced analytical methods. My technical execution involved designing, deploying, and analyzing comprehensive pre- and post-training tests, monitoring nuanced clinical performance metrics, evaluating facility utilization rates, and deeply analyzing concrete patient outcome metrics. Recognizing the necessity of understanding enduring educational outcomes, I developed specialized tracking methods for complex longitudinal studies. These methodologies allowed me to systematically track the long-term impact of our simulation training on actual clinical practices and direct patient care by seamlessly combining rigorous quantitative data tracking with rich, qualitative feedback mechanisms.
