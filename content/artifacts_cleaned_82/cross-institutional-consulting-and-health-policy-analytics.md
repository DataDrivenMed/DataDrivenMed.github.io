Cross-Institutional Consulting & Health Policy Analytics
Executive Consultation & Strategic Data Insights Beyond my direct operational oversight at LSU Health New Orleans, I have served as a strategic data consultant for Louisiana’s premier regulatory and academic bodies, including the State Department of Health, the State Medical Licensing Board, the LSU Healthcare Division, LSU A&M, and LSU Health Shreveport. Functioning as an objective subject matter expert, I provide customized data strategy and in-depth analytical insights tailored to the unique operational, regulatory, and strategic needs of each distinct organization.
Translating Complex Policy & Workforce Dynamics I leverage deep expertise in healthcare economics and medical education to demystify high-stakes domains for executive stakeholders across the state. I have partnered with these organizations to translate intricate datasets into comprehensive, actionable intelligence. Specifically, I provide the targeted data and contextual frameworks necessary to help leadership deeply understand complex issues such as CMS Medicare funding mechanisms, Graduate Medical Education (GME) financing models, and longitudinal physician workforce distribution.
Driving External Decision-Making Through Advanced Analytics To empower policymakers and system leaders, I architect sophisticated analytical models that transform raw healthcare and educational data into strategic foresight. By delivering these in-depth, objective insights, I enable state licensing boards, health departments, and academic leaders to accurately navigate the evolving healthcare landscape. This consultative approach ensures that their critical decisions regarding state funding, regulatory compliance, and workforce planning are universally supported by robust, empirical evidence and a profound understanding of medical education dynamics.
