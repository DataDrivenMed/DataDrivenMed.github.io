## Summary

The LLM Technical Brief is a live public technical education artifact for academic health sciences faculty and staff who need a more rigorous understanding of how large language models work. It supports a bridge between non-technical AI literacy and implementation-aware governance by explaining core technical concepts in language that can be used by educators, researchers, administrators, and governance committees.

The artifact is intended to help faculty and staff understand why LLMs can be useful, why they can fail, and why safe implementation depends on evaluation, context, data sensitivity, human review, and workflow design rather than model enthusiasm alone.

## Strategic value

This artifact demonstrates the ability to translate technical AI concepts into institutional decision support. It supports an academic health sciences center by improving shared understanding between faculty users, technical staff, compliance stakeholders, research leaders, and operational decision-makers.

## Evidence boundary

This is a public educational and technical literacy resource. It does not approve use of confidential institutional data, PHI, EHR-connected workflows, or automated decision-making. Any operational or clinical use requires appropriate institutional governance and human oversight.
