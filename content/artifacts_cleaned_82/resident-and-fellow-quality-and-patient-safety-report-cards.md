## Original Source Content

In collaboration with LSU Health Care Services Division, I worked closely with informatics and analytics teams to design and implement one of the first resident and fellow quality and patient safety report cards during the 2013–2014 period. This effort leveraged HCSD’s established Healthcare Analytic and Reporting Platform (HARP) and its federated clinical data warehouse infrastructure to enable structured performance measurement at the trainee level.
The report cards were developed using defined measure sets aligned with clinical guidelines and national benchmarks, including metrics from Healthcare Effectiveness Data and Information Set and the Quality Payment Program. These tools allowed for multi-level performance assessment across organizations, facilities, clinics, and individual providers, extending that framework to graduate medical education.
This initiative represented an early effort to integrate quality improvement and patient safety metrics into trainee evaluation. While the model demonstrated feasibility and value, its long-term sustainability was affected by broader system-level changes, including the transition to a public-private hospital structure across Louisiana’s healthcare system.
