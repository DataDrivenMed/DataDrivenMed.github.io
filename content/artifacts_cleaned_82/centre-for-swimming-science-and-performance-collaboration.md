## Original Source Content

I have spent the past several years building the infrastructure for elite swimming science in India, working directly with the National Swimming Coach of India and the leadership of the country's top competitive swim programs.

The current project is the Centre for Swimming Science and Performance (CSSP), a proposed independent research and applied science center to be embedded at Basavanagudi Aquatic Centre in Bengaluru. The work involves designing the governance structure, funding model, staffing blueprint, and long-term operating framework from scratch — built to function without dependence on any single funder or institutional patron.

The founding scientific collaboration brings in Dr. Pralay Majumdar, a sports physiologist and academic with 30 years of experience in post-graduate sports medicine education and applied physiology. Dr. Majumdar has published research in international and national journals, has been a peer reviewer for the Journal of Physical Education and Sport Management, the International Journal of Swimming Kinetics, and the Annals of Applied Sport Science, and holds an honorary faculty appointment at Swami Vivekananda Yoga Anusandhana Samsthana (Deemed University). He received the Prof. S.R. Maitra Memorial Award from the Physiological Society of India in December 2000.

The collaboration spans the US and India across institutional, academic, and athletic domains — combining applied sports science, center governance, and Olympic-level coaching into a single founding team.
