## Summary

Enterprise AI is a live public strategy and governance artifact for academic health sciences faculty and staff. It frames AI adoption as an enterprise operating problem rather than a collection of isolated tools. The artifact supports leadership-level thinking about governance, use-case prioritization, data sensitivity, risk classification, workforce readiness, implementation boundaries, and evaluation.

The resource is relevant to academic health sciences environments where education, research, administration, and partner-governed clinical operations intersect. It helps position AI transformation as a managed institutional capability requiring policy, process, evaluation, and role clarity.

## Strategic value

This artifact demonstrates the ability to connect AI literacy, governance, operational strategy, and institutional transformation. It supports senior-level conversations about how an academic health sciences center can move from scattered AI experimentation toward a structured enterprise AI operating model.

## Evidence boundary

This is a public strategy and education artifact. It does not represent final institutional policy, approve operational deployment, or authorize use of confidential data, PHI, EHR-connected tools, or automated decision-making. Implementation requires appropriate institutional and partner governance.
