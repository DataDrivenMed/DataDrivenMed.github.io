MCIP Educational Platform Development & Multi-Hospital Quality Improvement

Led development of the Managed Care Incentive Payment (MCIP) educational 
technology platform for Louisiana Quality Network's 17-hospital statewide 
network. Partnered with Assistant Dean of Advanced Learning and Simulation 
to design and implement video-based learning modules addressing CMS-mandated 
quality milestones, beginning with cancer screening initiatives. Developed 
comprehensive pre/post-training survey instruments and coordinated with IT 
to build automated platform featuring video hosting, embedded assessments, 
data collection systems, and hospital-specific analytics dashboards. 
Platform enables systematic quality improvement measurement across partner 
hospitals while supporting state-approved Medicaid managed care quality 
initiatives affecting patient care statewide.
