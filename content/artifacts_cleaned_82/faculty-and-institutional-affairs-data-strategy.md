# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Institutional Policy
Operating within the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I employ advanced data analytics to comprehensively evaluate, inform, and enhance institutional programs and policies. My primary focus is on supporting the entire faculty life cycle, encompassing strategic recruitment, long-term retention, formal promotion processes, tenure acquisition, and overall performance management. I explore high-level strategic areas of interest critical to senior leadership. Specifically, I conduct deep-dive analyses on climate surveys, complex gender and salary equity studies, and comprehensive retention and attrition rates. I directly utilize these strategic analyses to inform broader institutional planning efforts and strengthen competitive grant applications. To ensure our institution remains competitive on a national scale, I perform extensive benchmarking and comparative analysis. I utilize various specialized tools and resources to strictly benchmark our internal metrics against aggregate data from other United States medical schools. This comparative approach allows me to systematically identify crucial opportunities for institutional improvement and set concrete, data-backed targets in key strategic areas.

## Technical Execution and Data Governance
To drive these strategic initiatives, I conduct comprehensive, rigorous analysis on complex faculty data sets. This includes evaluating detailed metrics such as academic rank, time in rank, demographic profiles, tenure status, and specific term appointment end dates. Establishing a robust data infrastructure is essential to my role. I actively lead data governance efforts by collaborating directly with designated data stewards across various offices and departments to ensure highly accurate, standardized, and accessible data collection and analysis. I am responsible for establishing clear, repeatable protocols for obtaining and analyzing data to efficiently fulfill common, recurring reporting responsibilities while simultaneously building the agile capacity to address complex, ad hoc reporting requests from institutional leadership. 

## Impact, Outcomes, and Reporting
I rigorously evaluate the real-world effectiveness of existing institutional programs and policies that support the faculty. I conduct strict, evidence-based analyses to definitively assess the success and measurable impact of various faculty initiatives, proactively proposing strategic improvements driven entirely by this analytical data. To democratize these insights, I generate tailored, highly visual reports and dynamic dashboards that effectively showcase key faculty and institutional metrics. These tools are designed specifically to empower informed decision-making by school administrators and the faculty at large. I systematically communicate my analytical findings, explicitly noting crucial data limitations and special contextual circumstances, directly to senior leadership. Through this transparent and continuous communication, I actively promote, build, and sustain a pervasive data-driven culture throughout the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs.
