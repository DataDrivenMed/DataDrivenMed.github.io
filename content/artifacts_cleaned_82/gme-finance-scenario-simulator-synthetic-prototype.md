## Original Source Content

The GME Finance Scenario Simulator is a live demonstration prototype built with Streamlit to show how graduate medical education finance assumptions can be converted into executive-facing scenario analysis.

This prototype uses sample and synthetic data only. It does not display confidential institutional, student, trainee, faculty, patient, hospital, partner, or financial records.

The tool models financial and operational impacts of trainee movements, payment-rate changes, and affiliation scenarios. It includes real-time waterfall analysis, sensitivity modeling, and board-ready comparison tables designed for senior leadership, finance teams, GME leaders, contracts teams, and health-system partners.

Strategic value:

- Converts complex GME finance assumptions into a practical decision-support interface.
- Shows how trainee distribution, payment assumptions, and affiliation structures can be tested across scenarios.
- Demonstrates executive product thinking at the intersection of GME policy, finance, contracts, and workforce strategy.
- Provides a public-facing sample of how institutional strategy can be translated into reusable analytical tooling.
- Uses synthetic demonstration data to protect confidentiality while still showing the structure of the analytic approach.

Core capabilities demonstrated:

- GME finance scenario modeling
- Affiliation and trainee movement analysis
- Waterfall visualization
- Sensitivity modeling
- Executive decision-support table design
- Streamlit product development
- Translation of complex policy and finance logic into usable leadership tools
