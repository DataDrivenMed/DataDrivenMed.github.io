# Resources, Technology & Innovation

## Strategic Execution and Resource Planning
I coordinated a critical, collaborative effort involving all clinical departments within the LSU School of Medicine (SOM) and the LSU Health Sciences Center's (LSUHSC) Office of Property and Facilities Management. This strategic initiative focused on assessing and addressing our departmental program space requirements, as well as conducting program-level tabulations, to support our proposed relocation from our current office spaces at 1542 Tulane Avenue to the former ILH (Interim LSU Hospital) facility. My coordination efforts were instrumental in ensuring that the clinical departments could seamlessly transition to the new location while fully maintaining the necessary spatial requirements for their operations and services. Furthermore, I provided essential data support for tracking healthcare providers who received benefits from the State Student Loan Repayment Program. In this capacity, I maintained comprehensive records, ensured strict compliance with program requirements, and facilitated the efficient administration of these vital loan repayment benefits.

## Technical Execution and Innovation
I coordinated the development and technical implementation of a video learning platform focused on cancer screening education. This platform integrated pre- and post-test surveys utilizing standardized patients tailored for the MCIP program. To ensure the platform met high educational standards and effectively enhanced learning outcomes for all participating hospital providers, I collaborated closely with the Associate Dean of the Center for Advanced Learning and the LSUHSC IT team throughout the entire development life cycle.
