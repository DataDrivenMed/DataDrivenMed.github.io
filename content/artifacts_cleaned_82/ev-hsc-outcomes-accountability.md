# HSC-Wide Outcomes & Accountability Framework: Academic Affairs Consultation

**Source evidence excerpt:** Provided consultation to Dr. Porche, Vice Chancellor for Academic Affairs, on structuring an HSC-wide outcomes and accountability framework supporting accreditation, institutional effectiveness, CQI and emerging federal accountability requirements.

## Role and status

Advisor to HSC Academic Affairs. Consultation provided; framework development proposed.

## Scope and evidence

Proposed HSC Core layer: common measures across schools, including enrollment, retention, completion, graduation, placement, licensure and post-completion outcomes.

Proposed School/Program layer: profession-specific outcomes, including USMLE and Match for the School of Medicine, with equivalent measures for other HSC schools and programs.

Proposed Executive/CQI layer: internal diagnostic measures such as course performance, progression risks, surveys, remediation, time-to-degree, subgroup analyses and drivers of attrition. The metrics are not yet defined.

Proposed Financial/Federal Accountability layer: cost, debt, earnings and related measures, including determining which programs fall within applicable federal earnings-accountability requirements. Program applicability has not been established by this record.

Existing template and next step: identified the SOM institutional performance dashboard as a potential organizational and display template. The proposed next step is an SOM crosswalk mapping measures to applicable accreditation/federal requirements, data sources, owners, benchmarks and reporting uses. Crosswalk completion, HSC approval and implementation are not claimed.

Extends school-level accreditation and analytics experience into cross-school accountability design while preserving the distinction between consultation provided and a proposed framework awaiting definition and review.

This record reflects presenter-supplied information. Planned sessions are not represented as delivered. No attendance, satisfaction, learning gains, CME credit, institutional adoption or implementation outcome is claimed without supporting evidence.
