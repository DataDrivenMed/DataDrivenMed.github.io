# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Institutional Diversity
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, my primary focus for the Office of Diversity & Community Engagement involves harnessing data analytics to evaluate and strategically enhance diversity and inclusion. I systematically target improvements across student enrollments, admissions, graduate medical education, faculty, and institutional leadership to foster a more diverse and inclusive environment within the medical school. To proactively build our pipeline, I established robust relationships with various feeder undergraduate schools and developed targeted strategies to enhance the diversity pipeline directly into our medical program. Furthermore, I utilized data analytics to actively inform admissions strategies aimed at attracting a highly diverse and qualified applicant pool. Beyond the student body, I rigorously evaluated diversity within our faculty and leadership ranks, strategically recommending actionable measures for fostering a more inclusive professional environment. I also leveraged critical data insights to directly inform and guide our overarching community engagement strategies, fostering much stronger relationships with local communities and enhancing the medical school's external impact.

## Technical Execution and Data Analytics
To drive these strategic initiatives, I conducted comprehensive analyses of complex data pertaining to student enrollments, the admissions process, graduate medical education, faculty demographics, and leadership structures. By thoroughly examining this data, I effectively assessed the current state of diversity and inclusivity across the medical school. I utilized these data analytics to accurately identify historical trends, pinpoint critical gaps, and uncover specific opportunities for improving diversity across various facets of the institution. Additionally, I rigorously analyzed data stemming from feeder undergraduate schools to quantitatively evaluate their ongoing contribution to the overarching diversity of our medical school's student body. To ensure these insights were actionable, I generated tailored reports and dynamic dashboards that clearly showcased key diversity metrics and emerging trends.

## Impact and Cross-Functional Collaboration
My analytical framework and customized dashboards directly empowered informed, strategic decision-making by school administrators and the broader community. I consistently communicated my findings to key stakeholders, successfully promoting and sustaining a rigorous, data-driven approach to our diversity and community engagement initiatives. To achieve comprehensive alignment across the institution, I collaborated extensively with various cross-functional teams. Through this continuous collaboration, I ensured total alignment in our diversity and community engagement strategies while widely sharing the critical insights I gleaned from my ongoing data analysis.
