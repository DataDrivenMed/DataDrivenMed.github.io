## Original Source Content

SECTION: Institutional Research Data Analytics for Competitive Federal Grant 
Acquisition (May 2025)

Data Consultant, NSF EPSCoR CREST Center Proposal

• Provided comprehensive institutional research analytics for LSU Health 
  Sciences Center's successful $7.5 million NSF EPSCoR Centers of Research 
  Excellence in Science and Technology (CREST) competitive grant proposal 
  establishing the Center for Adaptive Nanometer Development (Award 
  #2538311)

• Conducted multi-dimensional workforce analysis of basic science faculty 
  including:
  - Comparative benchmarking against 150+ national medical schools
  - Regional peer institution analysis
  - Discipline-specific cohort comparisons using AAMC Faculty Roster data
  - Demographic and diversity metrics analysis

• Developed sophisticated tenure trajectory analytics calculating:
  - Median time-to-promotion (6.6 years Assistant to Associate Professor 
    for basic science faculty)
  - Promotion success rates by rank and discipline
  - 10-year longitudinal attrition patterns (33% for Assistant Professors)
  - Comparative retention metrics against national benchmarks

• Performed national competitiveness positioning analysis demonstrating:
  - LSU Health ranked 39th among public medical schools nationally
  - 74th among all U.S. medical schools
  - 22nd in Southern region for basic science faculty size
  - 102 basic science faculty (63rd percentile regionally)

• Analyzed junior faculty pipeline metrics revealing institutional gap in 
  Assistant Professor representation (26% vs. 33% national average), 
  providing critical workforce development justification for proposed 
  center investment

• Delivered actionable, evidence-based data products specifically praised 
  by Principal Investigator as "both informative and actionable" for NSF 
  proposal narrative development and competitive positioning

• Impact: Data analytics directly supported successful award of one of only 
  three NSF EPSCoR CREST Centers awarded nationally in 2025 ($1.5M annually 
  over 5 years), significantly enhancing institutional research 
  competitiveness and STEM workforce development capacity
