# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Legislative Consultation
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I assumed a primary role in supplying rigorous, data-driven insights to directly support state legislative bills. My high-level consultations spanned a diverse array of legislative focuses, specifically targeting complex healthcare workforce needs, the advancement of medical education, and the strategic optimization of financial and funding mechanisms for the state. By bridging the gap between raw institutional data and actionable public policy, I ensured that proposed legislation was grounded in factual analysis and aligned with the strategic needs of the medical community.

## Technical Execution and Medical Education Policy
I provided critical data support and analytical frameworks for major resolutions addressing the structure and future of medical training. For example, I informed the data strategy for HCR241 and HCR116, which urged the Department of Health and Hospitals and the LSU Board of Supervisors to conduct comprehensive studies assessing the current and future delivery of both healthcare and medical education in Louisiana to make targeted recommendations. I also contributed data insights for SR191, supporting a Senate Committee study focused on evaluating the governance, operational efficiencies, and service delivery of the LSU Health Care Services Division and the LSU Health Sciences Center. Additionally, my data evaluations supported HCR17, which created a specialized study committee tasked with evaluating and making systemic recommendations concerning Louisiana's overarching system of healthcare delivery.

## Financial Optimization and Funding Mechanisms
I utilized advanced data analytics to shape significant financial and operational legislation designed to maximize state and institutional resources. I guided data efforts for SB428, authorizing the LSU Health Sciences Center to strategically maximize affiliation agreements with other hospitals to fully leverage Medicare graduate medical education monies. My analyses also supported the creation of sophisticated, formula-based financing models for public graduate, professional medical education, and biomedical research through the Medical Education & Research Finance Work Group established by HCR134. Furthermore, I informed the development of outcomes-based funding formulas for postsecondary education under SB337 and the enactment of the Workforce and Innovation for a Stronger Economy (WISE) Fund via HB1033. I also supplied analysis for SB178, which established a fund to cover out-of-state tuition for students enrolled in specific medical education programs unavailable within Louisiana. 

## Impact on Healthcare Workforce and Hospital Administration
I leveraged my expertise in workforce analytics to directly inform legislative initiatives combating critical provider shortages and administrative bottlenecks. I supplied actionable data to support HCR83, a task force assembled to identify and address the severe shortage of specialist physicians across the state. I also provided insights for HR205, which organized a special committee to develop urgent strategies addressing the direct support professional workforce shortage, and HR230, which explored strategic means to enhance access to health services specifically within health professional shortage areas. In the realm of hospital administration, I guided legislative definitions and agreements, supporting SB98 and SB408 to explicitly define a "major teaching hospital" for the critical purposes of hospital prospective reimbursement methodologies. Finally, I provided analytical oversight for HB885, enacting the Safety Net Hospital Preservation Act, and HR42, which mandated strict approval processes for any cooperative endeavor agreements between the LSU Board of Supervisors and private entities involving a change in the management of public hospitals.
