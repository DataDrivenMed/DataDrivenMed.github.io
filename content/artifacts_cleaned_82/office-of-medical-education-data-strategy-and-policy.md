# Assistant Professor at LSU School of Medicine (2013-2017)

## Strategic Execution and Policy Development
Operating within the Office of Medical Education and the Department of Internal Medicine, I directly assisted the Associate Dean of Graduate Medical Education in identifying critical opportunities for the improvement of institutional reviews. This involved the strategic development, organization, and ongoing support of all residency training programs to ensure compliance and success within the complex framework of the ACGME Accreditation System. My focus was on achieving highly desirable, measurable outcomes for individual learners, specific programs, and the broader institution. To secure vital resources for these and other initiatives, I actively led and participated in rigorous proposal development and grantsmanship targeting both internal and external funding sources. Furthermore, I studied pressing healthcare workforce issues utilizing appropriate, data-driven methodologies. This research was pivotal in supporting informed, strategic decision-making processes for both the state's medical education community and high-level policy makers. I consistently communicated these workforce findings through impactful policy and action formats, oral presentations, visual displays, technical reports, and peer-reviewed publications.

## Technical Execution and Data Architecture
I optimized institutional data capabilities by extracting complex patterns and correlations using advanced predictive modeling techniques. By applying these sophisticated analytical methods across both structured and unstructured data infrastructures, I directly supported school administrators in making improved, meaningful, and highly informed decisions. To further democratize this data, I leveraged extensive, longitudinal learner evaluation records to engineer and produce tailored, aggregated reports and dynamic dashboards. These data visualization tools focused heavily on relevant educational metrics, effectively enabling enhanced, data-driven decision-making capabilities across the C-Suite, faculty leadership, and the learner population.

## Impact, Consulting, and Outcomes
Serving as a critical methodological resource for the department, I provided targeted consultation to faculty, residents, fellows, and staff regarding rigorous research designs, complex statistical analyses, and precise reporting standards related to medical educational research and systemic workforce challenges. I also acted as a specialized consultant for human patient simulation research projects. In this capacity, I focused heavily on evaluating clinical learning outcomes and effectively translating those research findings into practical, actionable educational strategies designed to tangibly improve the overall quality and safety of patient care.
