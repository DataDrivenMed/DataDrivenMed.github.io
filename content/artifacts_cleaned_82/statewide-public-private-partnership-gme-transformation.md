## **Statewide GME Transformation & Residency Asset Management**

*Collaborated directly with Dr. Charles Hilton (DIO) and LSU Health Care Services Division (HCSD) reimbursement specialists to provide the technical architecture for the statewide transition of GME programs from public to private management.*

- **Executive Office Coordination:** Served as a key technical resource during the negotiation of Cooperative Endeavor Agreements (CEAs), ensuring that GME priorities aligned with the strategic mandates issued by the **Governor's Office**, **the LSU Board of Supervisors**, Louisiana Department of health and the **Offices of Dr. Frank Opelka** (Executive VP for Health Care Redesign-LSU) and **Dr. Fred Cerise** (VP for Health Affairs-LSU).
- **Medicare Cap & FTE Engineering:** Masterminded the technical reconciliation and legal migration of **Medicare GME Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) caps** across the state. This included the high-stakes preservation of **109.34 caps** during the closure of Earl K. Long Medical Center and their subsequent transfer to private partners such as **LCMC Health, Ochsner Health System, and Our Lady of the Lake (OLOL).**
- **CMS & ACGME Regulatory Navigation:** Acted as the primary subject matter expert on CMS reimbursement regulations and ACGME institutional policies to ensure that federal funding remained uninterrupted and institutional accreditation was protected during the shift of hundreds of residents to private clinical sites.
- **Safety-Net Preservation:** Partnered with HCSD reimbursement specialists to draft and verify the technical data required for "Reversion Clauses," ensuring all GME assets and caps were legally protected as university property in the event of partnership dissolution.
- **Multidisciplinary Stakeholder Liaison:** Translated complex GME financial and accreditation requirements into actionable strategies for diverse stakeholders, including state legislators, university chancellors, and private health system executives.
