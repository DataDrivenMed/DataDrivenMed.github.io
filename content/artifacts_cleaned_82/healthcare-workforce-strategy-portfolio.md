# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Policy Leadership
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I actively drive healthcare and physician workforce strategy at the state and legislative levels. As an esteemed member of the Medical Education Commission (MEC), established by the Louisiana legislature in 1997, I contributed directly to formulating critical recommendations to the secretary of the Department of Health and Hospitals. My work focused on optimizing the distribution of funding for residency positions, residency supervision, and other vital medical education resources among the LSU HSC hospitals and medical education programs. I rigorously analyzed and proposed appropriate formulas for this funding distribution, significantly impacting the allocation of resources and ensuring optimum utilization for enhanced medical education and patient care. 

Furthermore, as a distinguished member of the Louisiana Healthworks Commission, I served as a collaborative force, integrating and coordinating resources relative to healthcare workforce development within various state departments and key organizations. I fostered collaborative efforts to synchronize these initiatives, promoting a more cohesive and effective approach to addressing workforce challenges in Louisiana. I actively engaged in the broader legislative process, utilizing rigorous data analysis to inform and shape bills, ensuring they accurately reflected actual healthcare workforce dynamics and addressed critical areas of need. I also contributed significantly to the formulation and implementation of major workforce programs, including the National Health Service Corps, HPSA designations, and Rural Healthcare initiatives. I engaged in an annual cycle of MEC meetings, issue discussions, and the production of public reports and publications, leveraging these platforms for the broad sharing of Graduate Medical Education (GME) data and overarching medical education trends both nationally and in Louisiana.

## Technical Execution and Data Architecture
I led the comprehensive collection and robust analysis of Louisiana healthcare workforce data to deeply enhance the understanding of workforce dynamics and effectively inform public policy. To ensure comprehensive analysis, I utilized diverse, high-level data sources, including federal, state, and private databases such as the National Provider Identifier file, the AMA Physician Masterfile, and the BLS Occupational Employment Statistics. I conducted precise, data-driven projections regarding healthcare workforce supply and demand across various geographies and medical specialties, accurately identifying potential shortage areas and proactively proposing targeted mitigation strategies. I continuously enhanced our underlying data collection methodologies and analysis processes, ensuring the highest levels of accuracy and relevance in the data utilized for strategic policy development. By integrating this key data, I directly informed high-level strategic healthcare workforce decisions at the LSU School of Medicine (SOM), the LSU Health Sciences Center (LSUHSC), and the LSU Systems Office, tangibly enhancing the overall effectiveness and distribution of healthcare education and clinical services.

## Impact, Research, and Outcomes
I spearheaded pivotal research initiatives exploring the direct impact of workforce shortages on patient care, the measurable effectiveness of existing workforce programs, and the complex factors influencing health workers' locational decisions. I disseminated these key research findings through various impactful channels, including detailed reports, strategic briefs, and formal presentations, providing invaluable, data-backed insights to policymakers and key stakeholders. To ensure the utmost data accuracy, reliability, and comprehensive analysis, I collaborated extensively with a broad network of professional associations, academic institutions, and independent research organizations. By continuously engaging with a broad spectrum of stakeholders, I actively fostered informed decision-making and policy development to systematically address healthcare workforce challenges. Throughout these efforts, I stayed meticulously abreast of emerging trends and issues within the healthcare workforce domain, ensuring LSUSOM's continuous adaptability and maintaining its forefront position in the field.
