# Oral Presentations

## Public Health and Preventive Medicine
Throughout my career, I have presented actionable public health and chronic disease research at major regional and state conferences. In April 2003, I gave an oral presentation on the "Illinois Breast & Cervical Cancer program and Illinois WISEWOMAN programs" at the Sangamon County Department of Public Health in Springfield, IL. The following month, I presented an overview of the Illinois WISEWOMAN Program's Pharmaceutical Assistance project at the Illinois Department of Aging, and delivered an implementation talk at the DuPage County Health Department's Office of Women’s Health. In August 2003, I provided an overview of the Illinois WISEWOMAN cardiovascular intervention program at the annual meeting of the Illinois State Diabetes Prevention and Control program within the Illinois Department of Public Health. In April 2004, I presented an analysis of county trends in obesity and overweight among Illinois adults using Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System data at the 63rd Annual Illinois Public Health Association Conference in Bloomingdale, IL.

## Simulation-Based Training and Patient Safety
I have disseminated extensive research on high-fidelity simulation in team training and operating room patient safety at national and international conferences. In October 2007, I presented on the feasibility of conducting high-fidelity, simulation-based interdisciplinary OR team training at the point of care during the Annual Meeting of the American College of Surgeons in New Orleans, LA. In February 2008, I discussed the initial implementation of mixed reality simulation at the Medicine Meets Virtual Reality Conference in Long Beach, CA. This work was supplemented by presentations on high-fidelity simulation and attitudinal changes in OR personnel at the American College of Surgeons Annual Clinical Congress in San Francisco, CA, and the Southeastern Surgical Congress in Atlanta, GA. 

I was invited to speak on the initial impact of the System for Teamwork Effectiveness and Patient Safety (STEPS) on the culture of patient safety at the International Forum on Quality and Safety in Health Care in Berlin, Germany (March 2009). My methodological work regarding debriefing was presented at the SERF Annual Symposium in San Antonio, TX, and resulted in the presentation of the Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing (OSAD) at the 2011 meeting of the Association for Surgical Education (ASE).

## Academic-Community Partnerships and Healthcare Quality
I presented on educational program development, clinical rotations, and manpower analysis at academic and medical meetings across the nation. In June 2009, I presented on creating value-added learner rotations in community health settings at the Louisiana Primary Care Association Conference, and followed this with an eight-step model presentation at the International Association of Medical Science Educators (IAMSE) Annual Meeting in New Orleans, LA. My work on healthcare workforce analysis was highlighted in February 2012 at the American Federation for Medical Research southern regional meeting, where I addressed the shortage of hematology/oncology specialists. Furthermore, I presented on the EQuIP program at the AAMC's Integrating Quality Meeting in 2013, focusing on educational approaches to quality improvement and patient safety.
