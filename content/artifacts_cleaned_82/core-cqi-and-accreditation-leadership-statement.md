## Original Source Content

Building on my tenure directing strategic outcomes—where I recently served as the CQI Lead for a successful LCME medical school reaccreditation and secured full ACGME accreditation for our sponsoring institution and 70+ GME programs—my vision as Assistant/Associate Dean of Continuous Quality Improvement is to scale this proven, systems-based architecture across the entire educational continuum. While achieving accreditation milestones is a critical baseline, my ultimate focus extends far beyond compliance to directly driving student outcomes and monitoring overall institutional effectiveness. By systematically triangulating over 90 distinct internal and external data streams—ranging from AMCAS and AAMC Graduation Questionnaires for UME, to ACGME CLER data for GME, and NIH RePORTER metrics for Research—I leverage frameworks such as Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) cycles and Lean Six Sigma to conduct rigorous accreditation "gap analyses" and root-cause assessments. We operationalize these insights by deploying real-time executive dashboards, generating dynamic process maps to eliminate redundancies, and publishing comprehensive annual quality reports aimed at supporting improved performance in key areas and monitoring long-term success indicators. Ultimately, this framework empowers all administrative and educational offices to pivot from reactive problem-solving to proactive enhancement, contributing directly to the sustainability, operational excellence, and overall effectiveness of our training programs.
