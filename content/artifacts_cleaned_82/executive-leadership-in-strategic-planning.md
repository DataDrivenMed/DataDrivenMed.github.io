Executive Leadership in Strategic Planning & Continuous Quality Improvement

Orchestrating Institutional Vision and Stakeholder Alignment
In direct partnership with the Interim Dean, I co-led the medical school’s comprehensive strategic planning initiative, acting as the primary liaison between executive leadership, clinical partners, and external management consultants. To ensure a cohesive and highly actionable institutional roadmap, I facilitated a complex, matrixed process that synthesized qualitative and quantitative insights from over 1,200 stakeholders—including faculty, residents, students, and health system executives. By embedding myself within the core clinical, research, and educational workgroups, I ensured that the resulting strategic priorities were directly aligned with evolving healthcare workforce demands, complex educational missions, and optimal resource allocation.

Driving Proactive CQI and Accreditation Excellence
I operationalize this strategic vision by integrating it with a rigorous, systems-based Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI) infrastructure. Leveraging frameworks such as Lean Six Sigma and Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) cycles, I systematically triangulate over 90 distinct internal and external data streams to conduct comprehensive gap analyses and root-cause assessments across the educational continuum. This proactive, data-driven approach shifts the institutional culture from reactive compliance to continuous enhancement—a strategy that recently culminated in my successful tenure as CQI Lead for our LCME medical school reaccreditation, alongside securing full ACGME accreditation for our sponsoring institution and over 70 individual GME programs.

Architecting Data Infrastructure for Transparent Accountability
To ensure the strategic plan remains a dynamic, living initiative rather than a static report, I architected the institution’s performance monitoring systems utilizing advanced data analytics and visualization tools. I own the longitudinal tracking of our institutional effectiveness, designing, deploying, and maintaining both internal executive dashboards and public-facing outcome trackers. By translating complex data sets into accessible Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), I provide leadership with the real-time, transparent insights required to monitor long-term student success, drive programmatic sustainability, and support agile, evidence-based decision-making at the highest levels.
