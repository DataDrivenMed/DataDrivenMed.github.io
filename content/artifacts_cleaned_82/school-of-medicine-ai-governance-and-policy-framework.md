## Role description

**Director of Strategic Outcomes & Accreditation**
LSU Health New Orleans School of Medicine

Leads accreditation strategy and institutional outcomes reporting for a School of Medicine operating across a complex multi-hospital training environment that includes LCMC Health, Ochsner Health, and FMOL Health System. Serves as the school's primary lead on AI governance in medical education, a role that developed in response to the absence of any SOM-specific framework governing how students, residents, and faculty use AI tools at the bedside and in assessment.

Drafted a pre-final School of Medicine AI policy and governance framework intended to guide SOM leadership, inform LSUHSC-level discussion, and provide a practical starting architecture for responsible AI use across medical education, clinical learning environments, data protection, tool evaluation, and faculty/staff guidance. The draft addresses educational behavior and clinical training questions that are not fully resolved by broad institutional IT or compliance rules.

Operates as an institutional data and AI subject matter resource, synthesizing regulatory developments  -  including Governor Landry's Executive Order 25-109, White House AI legislative recommendations, AAMC guidance, and OCR enforcement trends  -  into actionable policy language for non-technical audiences including program directors, department chairs, and the Dean's office.

## Accomplishments

- Developed a multi-document pre-final SOM AI governance package, including an initial policy draft, clause-level compliance audit against institutional documents, a revised non-duplicative policy draft, an updated white paper for program and fellowship directors, a Dean briefing memo with specific action requests, and a staff meeting script. The package is best understood as a leadership-ready draft framework for review, discussion, and refinement before final institutional adoption.

- Authored a white paper on AI in clinical training distributed to program directors, fellowship directors, clerkship directors, and department chairs  -  synthesizing HIPAA enforcement data, ACGME curriculum requirements, Louisiana executive order compliance gaps, and AAMC deskilling research into a single decision-ready document.

- Identified and documented a governance gap specific to academic medical centers operating across multiple affiliate health systems: trainees rotating through Ochsner, LCMC, and FMOLHS are subject to three separate AI governance frameworks with no mechanism to ensure they know which rules apply at any given site. Proposed a structural fix.

- Designed the recommended SOM AI Education Working Group structure  -  explicitly subordinate to the institutional AI Committee where the SOM already holds seats, and scoped to educational behavior rather than institutional compliance, avoiding the creation of a redundant parallel committee.

- Tracked and synthesized the March 2026 White House national AI legislative framework and its direct effect on pending Louisiana AI healthcare bills, translating federal-state preemption dynamics into plain language for SOM leadership within days of the document's release.

- Manages ongoing accreditation strategy across a medical school with training distributed across New Orleans' major health systems, with direct engagement with Dean Richard DiCarlo on governance structure and institutional positioning.
