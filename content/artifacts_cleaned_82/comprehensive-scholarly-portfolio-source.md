# **Curriculum/Model Development:**

Development of an Evidence-Based Model for Academic and Community Health Center Collaboration; Chauvin S, **Paragi R**

Collaborated to develop an evidence-based, systematic model designed to enhance collaborations between academic institutions and community health centers, specifically targeting academic residency and clinical rotations within Louisiana's Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs). This model provides a standardized framework for implementing academic rotations in FQHCs. It is crafted to be adaptable and applicable by academic and community health center leaders nationwide, aiming to establish effective rotations and community-based learning experiences. This includes the incorporation of inter-professional learning teams and the enhancement or expansion of existing educational programs.
