## Original Source Content

## **EXPERIENCE**

## **Director of Strategic Outcomes & Accreditation | LSU Health Sciences Center, New Orleans**

- Presented NIH research portfolio intelligence analysis to LSUHSC Chancellor, Dean, and senior research leadership, translating multi-year federal funding data into institutional growth strategy
- Applied Ansoff Matrix to LSUHSC's NIH award history, mapping four strategic growth vectors — funding depth with existing ICs (NIAAA, NCI), outreach to untapped funding agencies, new research area development, and full diversification — to inform the Dean's Office research enterprise planning
- Executed K-Means machine learning clustering (5-cluster, Elbow Method validated) on 200+ NIH project titles spanning FY2018–2022, identifying LSUHSC's dominant research domains and surfacing underfunded thematic gaps for leadership attention
- Conducted competitive NIH funding benchmarking of LSUHSC against Tulane University, comparing IC-level award distribution, department funding equity, and research concentration — delivered directly to medical school and research leadership as a positioning brief
- Produced executive SWOT analysis of LSUHSC's NIH portfolio, identifying funding concentration risk (heavy NIAAA reliance), departmental silos limiting crossdisciplinary grant potential, and specific ICs — including NCATS and Fogarty where LSUHSC had engagement gaps relative to peer institutions
- Recommended five cross-cluster collaboration targets to strengthen future grant applications, including Cancer + Genetics pairing and Environmental Health + Stress/Alcohol linkage, based on national NIH funding trend analysis
