# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Institutional Leadership
Operating within the Dean's Office, and specifically focusing on the Office of Graduate Medical Education at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I support the comprehensive mission domain of the institute. I actively contribute to attaining and renewing educational program accreditation, shaping patient care policies, driving quality improvement, conducting programmatic research to enhance scholarly productivity, and addressing community needs by networking with various programs and service organizations. I assist the Associate Dean of Graduate Medical Education in identifying targeted opportunities to improve institutional reviews. This involves supporting all aspects of our residency-training programs within the complex context of the ACGME Accreditation System to ensure desirable outcomes for individual learners, specific programs, and the broader institution. Furthermore, I support overarching leadership accomplishments by contributing to innovative strategies for attaining crucial program linkages and optimizing medical education outcomes. My strategic oversight directly encompasses the execution of Annual Program Reviews, the management of ACGME Citation Reviews, and the administration of Resident Surveys and extensive manpower studies. To further advance these institutional initiatives, I lead and participate in rigorous proposal development and grantsmanship efforts designed to secure vital internal and external funding sources.

## Technical Execution and Data Analytics
I utilize advanced data analytics, predictive modeling, and rigorous research methodologies to directly inform strategic decision-making and enhance both educational and patient care outcomes across the entire medical education continuum. I optimize institutional data by extracting complex patterns and correlations using advanced predictive modeling techniques. By applying these sophisticated analytical methods to both structured and unstructured data infrastructures, I directly support school administrators in making improved, meaningful, and highly informed decisions. I leverage extensive learner evaluation data to engineer and produce tailored, aggregated reports and dynamic dashboards focused on relevant educational metrics. These visualization tools effectively empower informed decision-making across the C-Suite, faculty leadership, and the learner population. Additionally, I conduct in-depth studies on complex healthcare workforce issues using appropriate data-driven methods. As a methodological expert, I provide dedicated consultation to faculty, residents, fellows, and staff regarding rigorous research designs, complex statistical analyses, and precise reporting standards related to medical educational research and workforce challenges. I also provide specialized consultation for human patient simulation research projects, working to efficiently translate these research outcomes into tangible improvements in the overall quality of patient care.

## Impact and Outcomes
My analytical framework and dedicated research efforts directly support informed, highly strategic decision-making by the state's medical education community and high-level policymakers. I systematically communicate my healthcare workforce findings and educational research insights through a variety of impactful channels, including policy and action formats, formal oral presentations, detailed technical reports, and peer-reviewed publications. By seamlessly integrating advanced data optimization with rigorous accreditation support, I ensure the continuous, evidence-based enhancement of our institutional programs, directly supporting the sustained success of our residents and the overarching academic standing of the medical school.
