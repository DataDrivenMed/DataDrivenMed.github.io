## Original Source Content

SITUATION:
"Reimbursement Solutions LLC is a healthcare finance consulting firm that 
hospitals hire for Medicare cost reporting and GME funding optimization. Their 
consultant was supporting one of our partner hospitals on Medicare DGME CAPs. This required technical expertise on DGME caps 
transfer mechanics and historical MOU documentation analysis."

TASK:
"Our Contracts Officer connected them to me as the recognized subject matter 
expert, noting I had been handling Medicare GME caps administration 'for many 
many years.' I needed to provide expert technical advisory to support their 
client engagement."

ACTION:
"I immediately demonstrated domain expertise and provided technical guidance on DGME caps transfer 
mechanics, analyzed the historical MOU documentation and CEA agreements, 
validated the caps transfer commitment requirements, and explained the Medicare 
cost reporting implications. This enabled the consultant to firmly support the hospital's 
position and ensure they wouldn't deviate from the caps transfer agreement."

RESULT:
"The consultant successfully validated the DGME caps transfer, protecting the 
hospital's Medicare GME funding. This demonstrated that my technical expertise is 
recognized and valued externally - not just within my institution. It also showed 
my ability to apply deep institutional knowledge to solve complex regulatory and 
financial challenges for external parties, which is essentially what consulting 
firms do."

COMPETENCIES DEMONSTRATED:
✓ Subject Matter Expertise (Medicare GME financing)
✓ External Consulting Advisory
✓ Technical Problem-Solving (complex regulatory issue)
✓ Institutional Knowledge Application
✓ Healthcare Finance Expertise
✓ Regulatory Documentation Analysis
✓ Client Support (indirect - supported consultant supporting hospital)
