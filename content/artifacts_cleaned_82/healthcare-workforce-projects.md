# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Workforce Development
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I focus heavily on healthcare workforce development through deep, systemic workforce analysis. I provided extensive data support to the State Area Health Education Centers (AHEC) to directly drive competitive grant applications concerning LSU New Orleans (LSUNO) and the broader State Primary Care workforce. I meticulously tailored this critical information to meet the specific, unique needs of both the Southeast and Southwest AHEC programs. Furthermore, I delivered comprehensive reporting to high-level stakeholders, compiling and analyzing in-depth data on Louisiana's state physician specialty workforce specifically for LSUHSC's Vice Chancellor for Clinical Affairs. This targeted analysis served as a critical resource for the Vice Chancellor, directly informing strategic planning, complex resource allocation, and essential partnerships with regional healthcare organizations.

## Technical Execution and Data Analytics
To build a holistic view of the state's healthcare landscape, I executed rigorous data analytics on Louisiana's physician workforce. I compiled and explicitly tailored this complex dataset for the Louisiana State Board of Medical Examiners' official reporting and presentations. This technical package incorporated vital metrics such as granular physician-to-population ratios, geographic distribution maps, specialty breakdowns, and nuanced trends in both licensing and practice patterns. I also conducted a multi-faceted, dual-layered analysis of the State Physician Workforce paired with LSUHSC's Graduate Medical Education (GME) statistics to inform the LSU Systems Office. This approach delved into state-wide geographic disparities while simultaneously analyzing internal institutional GME statistics, such as match rates, specialty choices, and overarching program effectiveness. Additionally, I compiled and analyzed specific GME data from the LSU Health Sciences Center in New Orleans to fulfill and exceed LSU's annual reporting requirements to the Systems Office. 

## Impact, Funding, and Outcomes
My data-driven insights served as the foundational element for external AHEC grant applications, directly aiding in securing vital funding aimed at enhancing medical education and systematically addressing primary care healthcare disparities across the state. By delivering a comprehensive view of both the statewide healthcare ecosystem and the institution's distinct role within it, my analytical findings were instrumental in shaping high-level strategic planning, policy formulation, and resource allocation decisions at the LSU Systems Office. Moreover, my targeted specialty workforce analysis directly contributed to the Vice Chancellor's overarching goal of enhancing specialized medical care availability and delivery across Louisiana.
