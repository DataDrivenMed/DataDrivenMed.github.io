# Enterprise Institutional Effectiveness Dashboard

**Portfolio category:** Analytics, Dashboards, and Decision Intelligence

**Portfolio role:** Live demonstration prototype using synthetic data

**Tags:** institutional effectiveness, executive analytics, synthetic data prototype, Streamlit, Plotly

**Source evidence excerpt:** Interactive demonstration dashboard showing how academic medical center leaders can view education outcomes, research enterprise indicators, workforce analytics, and accreditation compliance signals in one executive decision-support interface.

## Original Source Content

The Enterprise Institutional Effectiveness Dashboard is a live demonstration prototype built with Streamlit and Plotly to show how academic medical center leadership can view institutional performance across multiple domains in a single executive interface.

This prototype uses sample and synthetic data only. It does not display confidential institutional, student, trainee, faculty, patient, hospital, partner, or financial records.

The dashboard consolidates education outcomes, research enterprise indicators, workforce analytics, and accreditation compliance signals into one decision-support view. It is designed to show how fragmented reporting streams can be converted into an integrated institutional effectiveness architecture for senior leadership.

Strategic value:

- Demonstrates a unified executive view across education, research, workforce, and accreditation domains.
- Shows how institutional effectiveness can be monitored through decision-ready indicators rather than disconnected reports.
- Translates strategic planning and accreditation monitoring into an interactive leadership dashboard.
- Demonstrates applied product thinking using Streamlit and Plotly.
- Uses synthetic demonstration data to protect confidentiality while still showing the structure of the analytic approach.

Core capabilities demonstrated:

- Institutional effectiveness dashboard design
- Executive analytics
- Education outcomes monitoring
- Research enterprise indicators
- Workforce analytics
- Accreditation compliance signaling
- Streamlit and Plotly product development
- Cross-domain decision-support architecture
