# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Legislative Policy
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I lead comprehensive data initiatives that directly shape state healthcare laws, regulations, and policies. I provided extensive data support for the "Louisiana Vision for Support by Medicaid of Graduate Medical Education," formulating a strategic data package that encompassed key statistics on Undergraduate Medical Education (UME), Graduate Medical Education (GME), and the state's physician workforce. This foundational evidence was crucial in informing the Department of Health Secretary's overarching strategic vision for long-term planning and resource allocation. I also engaged directly with the LSU Systems team to play a pivotal role in advancing Louisiana's Medicaid Expansion application, focusing intently on analyzing physician workforce supply and capacity to support this significant state healthcare initiative. 

To support targeted legislative actions, I delivered comprehensive empirical data specifically tailored for a State Representative drafting a bill on equitable funding for University Health Hospital at LSUHSC Shreveport. This robust foundation ensured that funding allocations aligned equitably with the healthcare needs of the Louisiana community. I also compiled a comprehensive physician workforce report specifically for the Acadiana Region's local Legislative delegation to inform their regional healthcare policy decisions. Furthermore, I engaged in ongoing data reporting for LSU Health Sciences Center's legislative presentations, directing continuous data support to the Assistant Vice Chancellor of Administration and Finance to inform institutional legislative advocacy efforts.

## Technical Execution and Advanced Analytics
I utilize advanced analytics and spatial mapping to dissect complex healthcare scenarios and demographic interplays. I conducted a highly targeted data analysis to support Fred Cerise's initiative on "Managing Care for High Cost Patients among Multiple Payers & Providers." By delving deeply into patient demographics, medical conditions, treatment costs, payer structures, provider networks, and care coordination efforts, I developed a comprehensive view of high-cost healthcare scenarios for the Department of Health Secretary. I also executed an exhaustive data and spatial analysis mapping State Senate, House, and Congressional districts against LSUHSC New Orleans and LSUHSC Shreveport's employees, students, alumni, and patient encounters. I translated this complex spatial data into a series of engaging infographics delineating district-level impacts.

Additionally, I performed comprehensive data reporting on the Louisiana Physician Workforce specifically for Senior Legislative Analysts on the Health and Welfare Committee. I examined granular metrics such as physician-to-population ratios, specialty distributions, and geographic disparities in healthcare access to inform the Committee's legislative planning. For the Assistant Vice Chancellor of Administration and Finance, I tracked dynamic key performance indicators, including match success rates, specialty preferences, program effectiveness, and complex fiscal metrics related to educational funding.

## Impact, Advocacy, and Economic Outcomes
My analytical frameworks consistently provide the empirical foundation necessary to drive effective policy formulation and demonstrate institutional value. The spatial analysis and infographics I developed vividly showcased demographic interplays and underscored the substantial economic impact of both LSUHSC centers on the state's economy through state and federal taxes, successfully articulating our socio-economic footprint to legislative leaders. My in-depth analysis of high-cost patients yielded actionable insights that equipped state leadership to optimize care management strategies, foster vital collaboration among payers and providers, and ultimately reduce healthcare costs for high-need populations. 

In the realm of medical education funding, I conducted an exhaustive analysis of Louisiana's GME landscape tailored for the Louisiana State Medical Society. By examining various federal and state funding sources and correlating them with physician distribution and regional healthcare needs, I provided a pivotal resource that equipped the Society with the data-driven evidence necessary to champion effective healthcare policies and advocate strongly for equitable GME funding across the state.
