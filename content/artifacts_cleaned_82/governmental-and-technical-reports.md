# Governmental Technical Reports

## Strategic Execution and Committee Reporting
I contributed directly to a comprehensive, multi-year sequence of formal technical reports on behalf of the Medical Education Commission, in collaboration with commission members including Pinsky W, Amedee R, Weise J, Hilton C, Chesson A, and Rigby P. My strategic execution involved compiling, analyzing, and structuring critical data regarding Graduate Medical Education (GME) and undergraduate medical education resources across Louisiana. These documents were designed to provide the Louisiana Department of Health and Hospitals with the necessary empirical foundation to inform policy formulations and optimize funding allocations for medical education. 

## Technical Execution and Analytical Continuity
The documentation effort spanned five consecutive editions of the Medical Education Commission's Annual Reports (from the 13th to the 17th Annual Reports, spanning 2010 to 2014). My contribution to the technical execution of these reports involved analyzing and reporting data regarding the distribution of funding for residency positions, residency supervision, and related medical education resources among the LSU Health Sciences Center hospitals and state programs. I ensured that the data provided accurate reflections of statewide physician workforce dynamics, educational outcomes, and current state healthcare resources.

## Impact and Outcomes
The annual reports served as a continuous, year-over-year baseline for state policy advocacy and legislative planning. By providing clean, structured data on the distribution of GME resources, these reports directly influenced the formulation of actionable recommendations to the secretary of the Department of Health and Hospitals, ensuring optimum utilization of resources for enhanced medical education and improved patient care across Louisiana.
