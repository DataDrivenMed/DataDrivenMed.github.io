## Summary

The LLM Faculty Brief is a live public education artifact designed for academic health sciences faculty and staff. It translates large language models from a general technology topic into a practical faculty-facing resource for understanding what LLMs are, how they behave, how they can support academic work, and why safe use requires attention to privacy, evidence, role, context, and human review.

The artifact positions AI literacy as an institutional governance need, not simply an individual productivity skill. It gives faculty a structured way to think about LLM strengths, limitations, appropriate use, and risk boundaries in teaching, advising, scholarship, clinical education, and administrative work.

## Strategic value

This artifact demonstrates the ability to translate fast-moving AI technology into clear institutional language for faculty and staff. Its value is not only that it explains LLMs, but that it does so in a way that supports governance, adoption readiness, safe experimentation, and shared vocabulary across an academic health sciences environment.

## Evidence boundary

This is a public educational resource. It does not authorize clinical deployment, PHI use, EHR integration, learner assessment decisions, or institutional policy adoption. Any use involving sensitive data, clinical workflows, student records, or partner-governed systems requires appropriate institutional review and human oversight.
