# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Institutional Accreditation
Operating within the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I lead the overarching data strategy for the Office of Admissions. I provide crucial support in the development and implementation of comprehensive admissions data strategies specifically aimed at attracting a highly qualified and diverse applicant pool. I continuously evaluate the effectiveness of institutional admissions policies and processes, recommending structural improvements based on rigorous data insights and predictive analytics findings. A critical component of my leadership involves ensuring that all admissions processes and policies maintain strict alignment with LCME accreditation standards through continuous monitoring and evaluation. I directly contribute to the preparation of complex accreditation documentation and reports, effectively showcasing our robust, data-driven admissions framework to regulatory accreditation bodies.

## Technical Execution and Predictive Analytics
I leverage advanced data analytics and predictive modeling to inform and enhance the entire admissions process, ensuring a highly strategic approach to student recruitment and selection. I conduct thorough, deep-dive analyses of complex admissions data to proactively identify emerging trends, systemic challenges, and strategic opportunities for improvement. To achieve this, I developed and currently maintain robust statistical models and machine learning algorithms designed to analyze comprehensive applicant data, including test scores, academic grades, and qualitative essays. By utilizing these predictive analytics tools, I successfully identify critical patterns and accurately forecast applicant success in the medical program. Furthermore, I generate comprehensive reports and detailed visualizations to clearly communicate these complex insights, translating raw data into actionable intelligence for key stakeholders.

## Impact and Cross-Departmental Collaboration
My analytical framework directly facilitates and empowers data-driven decision-making within the Admissions Office. Beyond the immediate department, I collaborate extensively with cross-functional teams to ensure total alignment in our overarching admissions strategies and to disseminate the critical insights gleaned from my predictive modeling efforts. I engage in continuous, productive dialogue with various departments across the institution to deeply understand their specific data needs and provide tailored analytical support. Through these collaborative efforts, I actively promote, build, and sustain a culture of rigorous, data-informed decision-making across the entire medical school.
