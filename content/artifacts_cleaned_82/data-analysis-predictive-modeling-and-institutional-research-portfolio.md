# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Institutional Research
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I direct complex data analysis and research initiatives to inform high-level strategic planning across the institution. I collaborated directly with executive leadership, including supporting the Vice Chancellor for Academic Affairs at LSUHSC by compiling and analyzing comprehensive workforce data on faculty and staff for a high-stakes presentation to the LSU System, thereby aiding in critical strategic decision-making. I also provided comprehensive data support for the University Medical Center of New Orleans (UMCNO) marketing brochure, highlighting the career trajectories, specialty choices, and geographic placements of physicians trained at Interim Louisiana Hospital (ILH)/Charity Hospital to underscore the institution's impact on healthcare delivery. Additionally, I supported a faculty member in their successful leadership project, which contributed directly to their subsequent appointment as Assistant Dean for Admissions. Extending my strategic focus to community partnerships, I managed the evaluation and data management for the Southeast Louisiana Area Health Education Center and the LA-SEARCH program (2009-2017), driving initiatives to improve the supply and distribution of healthcare professionals across the state.

## Technical Execution and Data Analytics
I utilize sophisticated statistical models and analytical frameworks to conduct rigorous quantitative research and predictive forecasting. I designed a sophisticated analytical framework to correlate the predictive value of old MCAT exam scores with the new MCAT format, enabling the Associate Dean for Admissions and the Admission Committee to effectively forecast academic performance and clinical skills for future cohorts. In a collaborative capacity, I created a Data Analysis Plan and assisted an external PhD candidate in a quantitative study assessing the predictive ability of MCAT scores on the pre-clinical GPAs of Black medical students. During the COVID-19 pandemic, I played a pivotal role by conducting comprehensive data reporting and forecasting using sophisticated mathematical models at the National, State, and Parish levels. I achieved precise predictions of COVID-19 trends by analyzing highly complex variables, including case rates, hospitalizations, ICU admissions, mortality, Effective Reproductive Number (Rt), demographic factors, mobility data, and genomic analyses. Furthermore, I conducted a multi-faceted Healthcare Market Value analysis for the New Orleans Region, synthesizing census data, healthcare workforce statistics, real estate trends, and business analysis to directly inform the LSUHSC-NO Chancellor and Vice Chancellor for Business Development regarding the strategic redevelopment of Charity Hospital.

## Impact, Reporting, and Outcomes
My robust data analysis consistently provides actionable insights that shape institutional policy, resource allocation, and public relations. I conducted a comprehensive analysis of the LSUSOM Simulation Center's usage, tracking frequency and exercise types among students, residents, and faculty to ensure its continued efficacy and shape its strategic direction. To directly inform statewide healthcare discussions, I conducted rigorous analyses of Louisiana's physician supply and demand—including a comprehensive Louisiana Medical Education and Physician Workforce Analysis tailored for the Louisiana Healthcare Quality Forum. By forecasting the state's healthcare workforce needs over the next decade and analyzing medical education enrollment and retirement trends, I provided essential policy recommendations to balance the physician workforce. Finally, I compiled and analyzed comprehensive medical school and GME residency match statistics, delivering multi-faceted reports to the Dean. I simultaneously tailored this complex data for media releases under the direction of LSUHSC's Director of Information Services, translating key performance indicators into easily digestible insights that successfully elevated the institution's profile and showcased its ongoing commitment to medical education excellence.
