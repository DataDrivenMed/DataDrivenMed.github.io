# Awards and Honors

## Professional Recognition and Scholarship
Throughout my academic and professional career, I have been recognized for my contributions to medical education, surgical training, and public health. In 2011, I was awarded the Paper of Distinction at the annual combined meeting of the Association for Surgical Education (ASE), the Association of Program Directors in Surgery, and the Association of Residency Coordinators in Surgery for my work titled, "Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing (OSAD) in Surgery: Identifying and Quantifying Best Practice." 

Prior to this, in 2010, I received the Outstanding Presentation recognition for the Medical Education Scholarship Award (MESA) from the AAMC for my project on "Using a Standardized Model for Creating Sustainable and Value-Added Academic-Community Partnerships and Learning Rotations." In public health research, I was named a Finalist for the Best Student Abstract at the 132nd American Public Health Association Conference in Washington, DC (2004) for my study examining "Trends in prevalence of obesity and overweight among Illinois adults from 1995-2002: Implications for healthy people 2010 objectives." Additionally, I was nominated for the Student Worker of the Year Award by the Illinois Public Health Association (IPHA) in 2004, and I was recognized as a Guest of Honor by the University of Mysore, India in 1999.

## Athletic Leadership and Excellence
In parallel to my academic achievements, I achieved a distinguished track record of high-level athletic excellence and leadership. I was honored by the State Swimming Association and the Indian National Swimming Federation for my accomplishments in swimming and water polo at both the national and international levels between 1988 and 1997. During this period of athletic competition, I served as the Captain of the Karnataka State Swim Team for 7 years (1987-1993). Furthermore, I was named the Best Sportsman of the Year for 8 consecutive years throughout my undergraduate and graduate studies in Bangalore, India (1987-1994).
