TRANSFORMATIONAL LEADERSHIP: ADMISSIONS TECHNOLOGY MODERNIZATION

In 2025-2026, I spearheaded LSU Health's strategic evaluation and implementation planning for an enterprise admissions management platform (AMP/ZAP Solutions), a mission-critical system supporting 3,000+ annual applicants and institutional LCME accreditation compliance. 

Recognizing that the vendor-provided scoping document contained significant gaps that could derail implementation, I conducted a comprehensive technical assessment from three integrated perspectives—strategic, data/analytics, and IT/security—identifying over 200 critical requirements requiring clarification before contract execution. This analysis prevented potential scope creep estimated at $50,000+ and timeline delays that could have jeopardized the 2026-2027 admissions cycle.

I designed and deployed a structured requirements elicitation framework covering 13 functional domains, translating complex technical specifications into actionable decision points for non-technical stakeholders. This work required building consensus across organizationally siloed departments (Admissions, IT Security, Student Information Systems, Finance) and navigating the political complexity of three independent affiliate health systems with separate AI policies and IT governance structures.

Beyond technical assessment, I established the change management infrastructure for successful adoption: faculty interviewer training protocols, staff workflow redesign strategies, and applicant communication frameworks. This holistic approach—combining technical rigor, stakeholder diplomacy, and operational planning—positioned LSU to execute a high-stakes technology implementation while maintaining institutional autonomy and accreditation compliance.

Key Leadership Competencies Demonstrated: Strategic planning, cross-functional collaboration, vendor management, risk assessment, technical translation for executive audiences, consensus-building across competing stakeholders, and project governance.
