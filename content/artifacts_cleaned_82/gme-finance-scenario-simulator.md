## Original Source Content

The GME Finance Scenario Simulator is a live demonstration prototype designed to show how complex graduate medical education finance questions can be translated into an executive-facing decision-support tool.

The simulator models financial and operational impacts of GME trainee movements, payment-rate changes, cap scenarios, and affiliation structures. It is intended to help leadership visualize how changes in assumptions can alter financial exposure, workforce flexibility, and partnership strategy.

Key capabilities demonstrated include:

- Real-time GME finance scenario modeling.
- Waterfall analysis showing how assumptions contribute to projected change.
- Sensitivity modeling for payment-rate and trainee-movement assumptions.
- Board-ready comparison tables for leadership discussion.
- Translation of complex GME finance logic into a practical decision-support interface.
- Public-facing Streamlit implementation suitable for rapid executive prototyping.

This artifact is a demonstration tool using sample/synthetic data only. It does not display confidential institutional, student, trainee, faculty, patient, hospital, partner, or financial records.

Strategic value: this prototype shows the ability to convert GME finance complexity into a practical leadership product for Deans, CFOs, GME leaders, contracts teams, and health-system partners. It demonstrates not only subject-matter knowledge in GME finance and affiliation scenarios, but also the ability to build tools that make strategic tradeoffs visible.

Live prototype: https://gme-finance-simulator.streamlit.app/
