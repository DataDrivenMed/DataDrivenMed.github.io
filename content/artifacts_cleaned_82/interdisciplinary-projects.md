# Interdisciplinary Collaboration and Strategic Partnerships

## Strategic Execution and Institutional Leadership
I actively drove high-level interdisciplinary collaboration by partnering with various departments, external institutions, and global professionals to advance medical education and healthcare policy. I collaborated directly with the Department of Health and Hospitals (DHH) Office of Primary Care and Rural Health, the Louisiana Primary Care Association, and numerous Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs) across Louisiana. Through these strategic partnerships, I developed community-based clinical rotations designed to provide medical students with critical hands-on experience in underserved areas, actively fostering a long-term commitment to primary care in these vulnerable communities. Expanding my collaborative scope internationally (2011-2013), I partnered with the Centre for Patient Safety and Service Quality (CPSSQ) at Imperial College in London, U.K., and the Gippsland Medical School at Monash University in Victoria, Australia. Through this global initiative, I developed highly effective debriefing techniques specifically optimized for experiential learning in operative rooms utilizing advanced simulation-based exercises. 

Furthermore, I led a major funding initiative by gathering and articulating the New Orleans Metro region's physician workforce and medical education statistics for a pivotal grant application to the New Orleans Business Alliance. I synthesized comprehensive data to craft a compelling narrative that underscored the region's healthcare workforce readiness, directly aiding in the pursuit of essential funding to bolster the local medical workforce. I also coordinated the meticulous survey data collection and reporting process for the National Science Foundation's (NSF) Science and Engineering Research Facilities survey on behalf of the LSU Health Sciences Center (LSUHSC). I ensured absolute compliance with this congressional mandate, gathering crucial data concerning the extent, construction, maintenance, renovation, and financial support of our institutional research facilities.

## Technical Execution and Data Analytics
To optimize clinical training logistics, I conceptualized and developed an advanced matching algorithm tool designed to streamline the assignment of clinical rotations for medical students at Children's Hospital. Tailored specifically for the Pediatric Department, my algorithm analyzed and balanced complex variables, including individual specialty interests, faculty availability, and intricate logistical constraints. Operating in a rigorous analytical capacity, I conducted a comprehensive faculty promotions and salary study for the LSU School of Medicine (LSUSOM) in direct collaboration with The Committee on Women’s Affairs. I utilized targeted data analytics to evaluate faculty promotions and uncover salary disparities within the medical school, maintaining a strict, evidence-based focus on institutional gender equity.

Additionally, I participated in a high-level, multi-disciplinary team alongside members from the E. J. Ourso College of Business, the Public Administration Institute, the Division of Economic Development, the LSU School of Social Work, and the LSU School of Medicine. Within this structure, I conducted an in-depth analysis of the future demand and associated costs for long-term support and services. I analyzed complex variables such as demographic trends, projected service needs, and broad financial implications to produce a comprehensive report submitted to the DHH's Office of Aging and Adult Services. I also conducted an extensive analysis of the workforce outcomes of alumni from the Bogalusa Family Medicine Residency Program, in collaboration with the Southeast Louisiana Area Health Education Center (AHEC) program, generating a data-driven report that provided a longitudinal view of alumni career trajectories, specialization choices, and geographic distribution.

## Impact and Outcomes
My advanced matching algorithm at Children's Hospital not only maximized the operational efficiency of rotation scheduling but also tangibly improved the overall educational experience for students by strictly aligning their clinical exposure with their long-term career interests. By analyzing the Bogalusa Family Medicine Residency Program alumni, I delivered a critical longitudinal resource that directly informed regional healthcare planning and drove actionable strategies to meet ongoing community healthcare needs. I replicated this analytical success by conducting comprehensive reporting on the pediatric physician workforce trained at the Children's Hospital of New Orleans, tracking alumni to assess the training program's overall effectiveness and its direct alignment with regional and national healthcare objectives. 

To further support pediatric healthcare, I compiled an exhaustive, specialized report on the pediatric physician workforce to directly fulfill the strategic informational needs of the LSU School of Medicine's Department of Pediatrics Program Director. By mapping the distribution, specialties, and explicit gaps in pediatric service, I provided a vital, data-backed tool that empowered the Program Director to drive strategic planning, enhance curriculum development, and directly address specific healthcare needs within the community. Finally, my collaborative analysis of long-term support services yielded highly actionable insights and robust policy recommendations, serving as a crucial foundational resource for the DHH in optimizing strategic planning and resource allocation for aging and adult populations across the state.
