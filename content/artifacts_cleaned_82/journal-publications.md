# Journal Publications

## Public Health and Preventive Medicine Research
Throughout my research career, I have investigated population health outcomes, lifestyle behaviors, and the adaptation of evidence-based interventions. In 2006, I contributed to "Adapting Evidence Based Behavioral Interventions for New Settings and Target Populations" published in *AIDS Education and Prevention*, which explored methodologies for modifying interventions for new demographics. In 2007, I presented work on the results of a multi-site EBI adaptation project for adult HIV+ men of color who have sex with men at the 2007 National HIV Prevention Conference. Furthermore, in 2008, I examined the intersection of lifestyle behaviors, obesity, and perceived health among men with and without a diagnosis of prostate cancer in a population-based, cross-sectional study published in *BMC Public Health*.

## Patient Safety and Simulation-Based Training
I was actively involved in investigating the efficacy of simulation-based training models, specifically in operating room environments and at the point of care. In 2008, I contributed to research published in the AHRQ volume, *Advances in Patient Safety*, detailing the utilization of the Mobile Mock Operating Room for point-of-care team training and examining the effectiveness of point-of-care debriefing sessions. My research on high-fidelity, simulation-based training was published in the *Journal of the American College of Surgeons* and the *Journal of Surgery* in 2008 and 2009, demonstrating how repetitive training at the point of care drives teamwork improvements and attitudinal changes among operating room personnel. Additionally, in 2012, I participated in a tri-continental study published in *The American Journal of Surgery* that identified best practice guidelines for debriefing in surgery. In 2013, I co-authored research in the *Annals of Emergency Medicine* on conducting hospital disaster drills using simulation technology.

## Health Workforce Dynamics and Clinical Productivity
I performed extensive research on workforce policy, physician distribution, and medical school trajectories. In 2012, I published an abstract in the *Journal of Investigative Medicine* analyzing the supply and shortage of hematology/oncology specialists, which was followed by a comprehensive study on the supply of these specialists in the *Journal of the Louisiana State Medical Society* in 2014. My work on Graduate Medical Education (GME) and manpower analysis continued through publications in the *Journal of the Louisiana State Medical Society* from 2015 to 2016, covering match trends, post-disaster physician supply metrics in the aftermath of Hurricane Katrina, and evaluations of the physician pipeline production. Finally, I contributed to global workforce analysis with an article published in *JRSM Open* in 2017 focusing on world medical school production trends.
