# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Curriculum Development
Operating within the Office of Undergraduate Medical Education at the LSU School of Medicine, I utilize advanced data analytics and predictive modeling to inform and significantly enhance our overarching educational strategies. I ensure a strictly evidence-based approach is applied to curriculum development, student assessment methodologies, and comprehensive program evaluation. I provide crucial, data-driven insights to directly inform curriculum development, guaranteeing complete alignment with institutional educational objectives and rigorous LCME accreditation standards. I continuously monitor and evaluate the curriculum and educational processes to ensure they maintain strict, ongoing alignment with these LCME standards. Furthermore, I contribute heavily to the preparation of complex accreditation documentation and formal reports, effectively showcasing our robust, data-driven educational strategies to external accreditation bodies. I systematically evaluate the real-world effectiveness of various instructional strategies and recommend targeted, actionable improvements based on my analytical findings.

## Technical Execution and Outcomes Analysis
To drive these educational initiatives, I developed and currently maintain sophisticated statistical models specifically designed to analyze complex student performance data. I leverage these models to accurately identify emerging trends and pinpoint specific areas requiring immediate improvement. I actively utilize predictive analytics to forecast student success metrics and proactively identify potential areas for targeted curriculum enhancement. I also conduct thorough, comprehensive evaluations of our internal educational programs to rigorously assess their overall effectiveness in achieving desired, predefined learning outcomes. Based on these complex evaluation findings and my continuous data analysis, I recommend and engineer strategic enhancements to these educational programs to optimize learner success and institutional efficacy.

## Impact and Cross-Departmental Collaboration
My analytical framework directly impacts the wider institution through extensive collaboration with cross-functional teams. I work continuously to ensure complete alignment in our educational strategies and to widely share the critical insights I glean from my data analysis and predictive modeling efforts. I engage in continuous, productive dialogue with other departments across the medical school to deeply understand their specific data needs. By providing this dedicated, high-level analytical support, I actively promote, build, and sustain a pervasive culture of rigorous, data-informed decision-making across the entire institution.
