**Source evidence excerpt:** Planned interactive session in the Faculty Development Series, Designing Your 2027 with Purpose and Intention, focused on practical generative AI skills for teaching, research and administrative work.

Planned format: live demonstrations and guided exercises using Microsoft 365 Copilot or comparable tools, with clear objectives, relevant context, constraints, iterative refinement and critical evaluation of outputs. Learning objective 1: identify an appropriate task in teaching, research or administration where generative AI may be useful, and define the intended outcome.

Learning objective 3: evaluate AI-generated outputs for accuracy, source reliability, potential bias, confidentiality concerns and appropriate professional use before incorporating them into academic workflows.
