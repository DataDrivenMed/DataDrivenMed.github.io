# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Institutional Planning
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, my primary responsibility within the Office of Research centers on utilizing advanced data analytics to comprehensively evaluate and enhance the institution's research enterprise. To actively inform our overarching strategic direction, I conducted a rigorous SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis to systematically identify the critical factors facing our research environment. I leverage these actionable insights to directly support the development and implementation of strategic plans aimed at advancing the research enterprise. A major focus of this strategic support includes driving targeted data initiatives designed to rapidly boost our institutional NIH funding rank from 92 into the Top 60. To ensure comprehensive alignment across these initiatives, I collaborate extensively with cross-functional teams, widely sharing the critical insights gleaned from my data analysis and SWOT assessments.

## Technical Execution and Grant Analytics
I execute deep technical analysis by conducting comprehensive evaluations of institutional research grants to accurately assess historical funding trends, success rates, and specific areas for improvement. I translate this data into actionable intelligence that optimizes grant applications and streamlines management processes, tangibly enhancing our overall likelihood of funding success. Additionally, I meticulously analyze complex data pertaining to faculty scholarly activities to evaluate overarching productivity, academic impact, and direct alignment with the medical school's core research objectives. To modernize and streamline this capability, I successfully centralized faculty research metrics to feed directly into robust CQI (Continuous Quality Improvement) dashboards. 

## Impact, Reporting, and Culture
By generating these tailored reports and dynamic dashboards, I effectively showcase key research metrics and emerging trends, directly empowering informed, strategic decision-making by school administrators and the faculty. Utilizing these insights, I actively recommend and deploy data-backed strategies that foster a highly supportive and productive environment for faculty research endeavors. I continuously communicate my analytical findings to key stakeholders, actively promoting, building, and sustaining a pervasive, data-driven culture within the Office of Research.
