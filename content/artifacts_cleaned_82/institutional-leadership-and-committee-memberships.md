# Institutional Leadership and National Committee Memberships

## Strategic Execution and Institutional Governance
At the LSU School of Medicine, I actively contribute to academic governance and programmatic excellence through my sustained service on pivotal departmental and school committees. Since 2014, I have served continuously on the Advisory Committee on Continuing Medical Education. I also provided long-term leadership as a Steering Committee Member for the Enhancing Quality Improvement for Patients (EQuIP) Program from 2012 to 2020. In 2024, I took on extensive advisory roles to shape the institution's future, serving concurrently as a Committee Member for the overarching LSU School of Medicine Strategic Planning Advisory Committee, as well as the specialized Clinical Workgroup, Research Workgroup, and Education Workgroup. To ensure ongoing educational compliance and excellence, I have operated as a member of the LCME Accreditation Steering Committee and the Quality Improvement and Accreditation Team since 2023. At the broader campus level for LSUHSC, I contribute my technical expertise as a member of the Managed Care Incentive Payment (MCIP) program’s Informatics Team, a role I have held since 2018.

## National Medical Education Leadership
On a national level, I maintain deep engagement with the Association of American Medical Colleges (AAMC) to drive data strategies and best practices across the continuum of medical education. Since 2024, I have been an active member of the AAMC Group on Student Affairs and the AAMC Assessment & Evaluation Communities. To stay at the forefront of educational infrastructure and institutional growth, I also serve on the AAMC Information Technology in Academic Medicine Community and the AAMC Institutional Advancement Community. Furthermore, I contribute to complex data strategies as a member of the AAMC’s Data-Driven Academic Medical Centers (DDAMC) Work Group and support administrative development through the AAMC Virtual Community for Staff Professionals Across the Continuum of Education (SPACE).

## Public Health and International Sports Governance
My commitment to strategic governance extends beyond academic medicine into public health and international sports administration. Early in my career, I engaged with national public health initiatives as a member of the Illinois Public Health Association (2003-2005) and the American Public Health Association (2004-2005). On an international scale, I have sustained long-term leadership in aquatic sports and anti-doping regulations. Since 1997, I have served continuously as the Vice President of the Integrated Council of Noble Swimmers in Bangalore, India. Additionally, since 2005, I have operated globally as a Doping Control Officer for International Doping Tests & Management (IDTM), headquartered in Lidingö, Sweden.
