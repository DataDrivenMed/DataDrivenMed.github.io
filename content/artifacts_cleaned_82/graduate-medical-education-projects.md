# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Policy Consultation
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I lead critical data initiatives focusing on graduate medical education, curriculum development, and broad educational programs. I consulted extensively on the strategic development of a comprehensive "white paper" focused on Graduate Medical Education (GME) within Louisiana. For this initiative, I actively explored and evaluated critical strategies for sustaining the statewide physician workforce while meticulously analyzing the increased supply of physicians—with a heavy emphasis on primary care specialties—required to adequately meet the complex healthcare needs of the state's Medicaid enrollee population. Furthermore, I completed a highly targeted analytical project that significantly aided the current Assistant Dean of Admissions in the successful execution of his LSU School of Medicine leadership project. This specific endeavor focused heavily on identifying key data predictors for medical school admissions, allowing me to provide valuable, data-driven insights designed to tangibly enhance the overall admissions process and systematically improve rigorous applicant selection criteria.

## Technical Execution and Outcomes Analysis
To ensure our educational offerings remain effective and relevant, I conducted an in-depth, rigorous analysis of the LSU School of Medicine's Continuing Medical Education (CME) activities. Through this comprehensive study, I evaluated critical key performance indicators, specifically isolating and measuring program attendance rates alongside overall participant satisfaction metrics. The resulting analytical report serves as a vital institutional tool for accurately assessing both the real-world effectiveness and the broad programmatic reach of our CME activities. By establishing this evaluative framework, my data directly informed and guided future strategic planning, ongoing curriculum development, and continuous quality improvement efforts across the medical school.

## Impact and Conference Dissemination
I routinely translate complex educational and workforce data into impactful narratives to elevate the institution's academic presence on a national scale. I supplied comprehensive data on Undergraduate Medical Education (UME), Graduate Medical Education (GME), and the broader physician workforce to support a high-level presentation at the 23rd National Conference on Primary Health Care Access. This critical presentation, titled "Providing Primary Care in the Aftermath of a Natural Disaster," was meticulously prepared to support the Chair of the Family Medicine Department. My deep-dive data analysis included complex key metrics on UME and GME match rates, statewide physician distribution, and emerging medical specialization trends. I strategically framed all of these data points within the critical context of disaster response and urgent primary care needs. Ultimately, this tailored, data-backed information served as the foundational element of the presentation, directly contributing to a highly nuanced understanding of the systemic challenges and unique opportunities inherent in delivering effective primary care in a post-disaster environment.
