# Research Associate at LSU School of Medicine (2010-2013)

## Strategic Execution and Program Leadership
I supported the comprehensive mission domain of the institute by advancing key initiatives across educational attainment, patient care, programmatic research, and community service. Specifically, I led efforts in attaining and renewing the accreditation of educational programs, shaping patient care policies, and driving quality improvement initiatives. I actively conducted programmatic research to enhance scholarly productivity while simultaneously addressing community needs through strategic networking with various service organizations. Furthermore, I bolstered the institute's leadership accomplishments by contributing to innovative strategies designed to secure critical program linkages and optimize medical education outcomes. My strategic oversight directly encompassed the execution of Annual Program Reviews, the management of ACGME Citation Reviews, and the administration of Resident Surveys and complex manpower studies.

## Technical Execution and Data Architecture
I developed and maintained a comprehensive Longitudinal Educational Database designed to systematically collect performance data across all levels, including programs, faculty, fellows, residents, students, and practicing physicians. I utilized this infrastructure to establish critical links between medical education methodologies and real-world patient outcomes, thereby supporting the entire continuum of the clinical learning process. To maximize institutional insights, I optimized complex data systems by extracting hidden patterns and correlations using advanced predictive modeling techniques. By applying these methods to both structured and unstructured data infrastructures, I directly supported school administrators in making highly informed, meaningful decisions. Additionally, I leveraged extensive learner evaluation data to produce tailored, aggregated reports and strategic dashboards of relevant educational metrics, which enabled significantly improved decision-making capabilities for the C-Suite, faculty leadership, and learners.

## Impact, Research, and Outcomes
I drove critical insights into the healthcare landscape by conducting extensive manpower studies, utilizing rigorous research methodologies to provide policy-relevant dissemination of workforce data to key policymakers and institutional researchers. I also provided specialized consultation for human patient simulation research projects, focusing on learning outcomes and the efficient translation of research data to tangibly improve the quality of patient care. To ensure the continuous advancement of these initiatives, I actively developed, led, and participated in rigorous proposal development and grantsmanship, targeting both internal and external funding sources. I served as a primary methodological consultant to faculty, residents, fellows, and staff, providing expert guidance on research designs, statistical analyses, and reporting requirements related to medical educational research and workforce challenges. I consistently disseminated these critical workforce findings and research outcomes through diverse channels, including policy-action formats, oral presentations, visual data displays, comprehensive technical reports, and peer-reviewed publications.
