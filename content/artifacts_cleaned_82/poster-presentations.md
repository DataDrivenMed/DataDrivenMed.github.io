# Poster Presentations

## Public Health and Chronic Disease Prevention
Throughout my research career, I have presented findings at major national and regional conferences focusing on chronic disease prevention, cardiovascular risk, and the determinants of obesity. In 2003, I contributed to the presentation of the Illinois WISEWOMAN Program at the 17th National Conference on Chronic Disease Prevention and Control in St. Louis, MO, co-sponsored by the CDC and Prevention Research Centers. In April 2003, I presented a poster on "Determinants of Obesity in Illinois Adult Population" at the 62nd Illinois Public Health Association Conference & Annual Meeting in Springfield, IL. In November 2004, I presented my work on "Trends in prevalence of obesity and overweight among Illinois adults from 1995-2002—Implications for healthy people 2010 objectives" at the 132nd Annual American Public Health Association Meeting in Washington, DC. Later, in April 2007, I presented a trend analysis on "Gender Differences in Cardiovascular Disease Risk Factors: Trend Analysis, 1996-2005" at the 66th Illinois Public Health Association Conference in Springfield, IL.

## Point-of-Care Simulation and Team Training
I have presented extensive research evaluating the impact of high-fidelity, simulation-based interdisciplinary training on operating room teamwork and patient safety. In August 2007, I presented work on the System for Teamwork Effectiveness and Patient Safety (STEPS) at the Gulf Coast Forum in Biloxi, MS. I subsequently presented initial evaluations of the training model at the Annual Conference of the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ) in Bethesda, MD, in September 2007. 

In January 2008, I presented a poster on "Conducting a Multidisciplinary Team Training Course to Improve OR Patient Safety at the Point of Healthcare with the Use of High Fidelity Human Patient Simulation" at the Annual Meeting for the Society for Simulation in Healthcare in San Diego, CA. Later that year, I presented on "The Influence of Point-of-Care Teamwork Training on Actual Operating Room Practice" at the AHRQ 2008 Annual Conference. In October 2009, I presented a poster focusing on "Reworking Rural Operating Room (OR) Education: Portability of Team Training Using the Mobile Mock OR" at the American Society of Anesthesiologists Annual Meeting in New Orleans, LA.

## Medical Education and Disaster Simulation
My research also encompasses the application of simulation technology to disaster preparedness and medical education. In March 2013, I presented a research and innovations poster titled "Conducting Hospital Disaster Drills Using Simulation Technology" at the 2013 CORD Academic Assembly, which focused on maximizing innovation, assessment, and educational outcomes in competency-based medical education in Denver, CO.
