# Grants and Contracts

## Strategic Execution and Healthcare Infrastructure
Throughout my career, I have secured, designed, and evaluated a broad portfolio of interventional studies, collaborative community grants, and major infrastructural investments. In 2002, I served as a Data Analyst for the interventional clinical study of the Illinois WISEWOMAN Program (IWP), delivering health behavior interventions to low-income, under- or uninsured women aged 40–64 to prevent and control cardiovascular and chronic diseases. In 2004, I served as a Data Analyst for the ADAPT-POL project in New Orleans, an observational study piloting CDC adaptation guidance for evidence-based behavioral interventions in Internet-based HIV prevention. In 2006, I managed the Evaluation of the System for Teamwork Effectiveness and Patient Safety (STEPS) project, an AHRQ-funded special emphasis panel to implement interdisciplinary, simulation-based training models in OR environments using mobile mock operating rooms (MMOR) and reflective debriefings. 

In 2009, I acted as Project Manager and Data Analyst for the Louisiana CHC Academic Residency/Rotation Model, creating a standardized, evidence-based approach for academic rotations and inter-professional learning teams across Louisiana's Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs). In 2013, I served as Co-PI for the Louisiana Greater New Orleans Community Health Center Infrastructure Investment Grant, collaborating with the Interim LSU Public Hospital to design and develop an interactive, dynamic dashboard of clinical outcome data to improve care for the Medicaid Waiver population at ILH. In 2018, I acted as a Consultant for the Louisiana Managed Care Incentive Program (MCIP), helping design the operations and steering committees alongside the informatics framework for the Louisiana Quality Network in collaboration with the LSUHSC-NO Vice Chancellor of Finance and the Louisiana Department of Health (LDH). Most recently, I served as the Evaluation Consultant for the LSUHSC New Orleans Center for Advanced Learning and Simulation (CALS) Project, supporting a $10 million U.S. House Appropriations Community Project Funding request to equip a 16-station Standardized Patient Project, a Simulated Emergency Department, a Wet Lab, and six simulated operating rooms.

## Technical Execution and Data Analysis
My technical responsibilities span clinical trials, CMS and GME cost reporting, and system-wide educational accreditation. In 2004, I designed and evaluated the Medtronic Corporation Patient Safety Course targeting Cardiac Implantable Electrical Device Right Sided Implant Training to improve future course implementation quality. Starting in 2007, I served as an Evaluator and Data Analyst for the American College of Surgeons Division of Education Program for Accreditation of Education Institutes (ACS-AEI) Level 1 Comprehensive Education Institute, ensuring standards for simulation-based surgical education and lifelong learning. 

For the MCIP program dashboard initiatives, I integrated diverse data streams, including LSU HCSD quality metrics and heterogeneous sources like EPIC, to provide detailed retrospective views of trainee clinical outcome measures. Additionally, I managed and reported on the complex regulatory aspects of CMS Medicare Graduate Medical Education funding caps across Louisiana for the LSU System Office, ensuring institutional financial viability in a dynamic regulatory landscape.

## Impact and Outcomes
My work has had a direct, measurable impact on state health policies, program safety, and health education funding. The STEPS project assessed how organizational factors influence the transfer of training to on-the-job team performance, leading to a culture of increased patient safety in the operating room. My collaborative policy-driven work surrounding CHC rotations, health professional shortage areas, and ACGME-accredited educational institutes continues to set the benchmark for healthcare workforce readiness in Louisiana.
