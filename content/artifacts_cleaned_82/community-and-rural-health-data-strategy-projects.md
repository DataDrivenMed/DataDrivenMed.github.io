# Assistant Professor and Director of Accreditation & Strategic Planning Compliance (2017-Present)

## Strategic Execution and Community Partnerships
Operating within the Office of Faculty and Institutional Affairs and the Dean's Office at the LSU School of Medicine in New Orleans, I spearhead targeted projects aimed at community outreach, public health, and vital rural health initiatives. I actively collaborated with the Louisiana Department of Health and Hospitals (DHH) Bureau of Primary Care & Rural Health to strategically validate Medically Underserved Areas/Populations (MUA/P) and Health Professional Shortage Areas (HPSA) across the state. Furthermore, I served as the primary liaison bridging academic and community healthcare leaders to facilitate an ongoing collaboration between LSUHSC-New Orleans faculty—specifically within the Schools of Nursing and Medicine—and executive leadership at Excelth, Inc. and the Jefferson Parish Community Health Center, a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC). Through this initiative (2009-2011), I successfully forged a new partnership and created a standardized academic rotation model in action, specifically designing an inter-professional rotation for Family Medicine residents and Nurse Practitioner students operating within the FQHC.

## Technical Execution and Predictive Modeling
To address critical rural healthcare provider shortages, I conceptualized and developed a sophisticated predictive model utilizing complex admission metrics to accurately forecast the likelihood of medical students entering rural practice. This model analyzed highly specific variables, including geographic background, academic performance, and extracurricular involvement, to calculate rural practice propensity. In tandem with this predictive tool, I engineered and implemented a comprehensive tracking system to follow rural track medical students longitudinally through to their eventual practice locations. Additionally, I collaborated directly with the DHH Bureau to build the essential data infrastructure required for assessing the current and projected supply of physicians in Louisiana. As part of this effort, I provided critical feedback and hands-on assistance regarding overarching data collection methodologies, comprehensive database preparation, and the execution of complex, policy-driven research.

## Impact and Outcomes
By combining predictive modeling with a longitudinal data tracking system, I established a dual-layered analytical approach that yielded highly actionable insights for both admissions strategies and ongoing program development. Executed directly for the Chair and Director of the Rural Track, this data-driven initiative successfully refined our admissions criteria, vastly improved the operational effectiveness of the rural track program, and significantly enhanced the program's long-term impact on rural healthcare delivery by capturing the actual career choices of our graduates. Furthermore, through my rigorous analysis of healthcare access data and population health metrics in partnership with the state, I successfully identified vulnerable geographic areas in critical need of increased healthcare resources, effectively leveraging this data to directly inform statewide healthcare workforce planning and critical policy development.
