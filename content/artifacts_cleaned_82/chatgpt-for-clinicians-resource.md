## Summary

ChatGPT for Clinicians is a live public education artifact designed to help clinicians, faculty, trainees, and health professions educators understand appropriate and inappropriate uses of general-purpose conversational AI in healthcare-adjacent contexts. It supports practical AI literacy by emphasizing evidence, uncertainty, role boundaries, human review, and patient-safety considerations.

The artifact is positioned as a governance-aware educational resource rather than a clinical deployment tool. It helps academic health sciences audiences distinguish AI-supported explanation, drafting, study, and workflow support from clinical decision-making, diagnosis, triage, or patient-specific care.

## Strategic value

This artifact demonstrates the ability to convert public concern about general-purpose AI in medicine into a structured educational and governance resource for clinicians and faculty. It supports safe adoption discussions by making risk boundaries visible and understandable.

## Evidence boundary

This is a public education artifact. It is not a clinical decision support tool and does not authorize use for diagnosis, triage, treatment, patient communication, PHI processing, or EHR-connected workflows. Clinical use requires appropriate institutional governance and licensed professional judgment.
