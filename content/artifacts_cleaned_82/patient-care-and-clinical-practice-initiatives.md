# Patient Care and Clinical Practice Projects

## Strategic Execution and Quality Assurance
I collaborated directly with LSU's Healthcare Service Division to conceptualize, design, and develop comprehensive Clinical Report Cards specifically tailored for Graduate Medical Education (GME) trainees. I established a structured evaluative framework that provided a comprehensive evaluation of trainee performance across various critical clinical competencies, including direct patient care, medical knowledge, and interpersonal skills. By utilizing a robust blend of quantitative metrics, I engineered these report cards to serve as a pivotal developmental tool for both trainees and supervising faculty. This strategic initiative enabled highly targeted feedback and continuous performance improvement, significantly enhancing the overall educational experience and actively promoting clinical excellence among all GME trainees.

## Technical Execution and Spatial Analytics
To drive data-informed decision-making at the executive level, I conducted detailed analyses and complex spatial mapping of patient encounters across the LSU Healthcare Network. I utilized advanced spatial analytics to accurately map geographic patient distribution, categorize specific types of medical encounters, and track complex utilization trends over time. I delivered these insightful visuals and comprehensive datasets directly to the CEO of the LSU Healthcare Network. Furthermore, I executed a detailed analysis of complex, patient-level data specifically for Interim Louisiana Hospital (ILH). I synthesized these intricate findings into a dynamic, interactive map designed exclusively for high-level strategic review by the LSU Health Sciences Center (LSUHSC) Chancellor. 

## Impact and Outcomes
My comprehensive mapping initiatives and interactive data visualizations successfully transformed raw clinical data into actionable executive intelligence. These tools provided a highly granular, easily digestible view of patient demographics, service utilization patterns, and clinical outcomes. By effectively illustrating the realities of patient care and clinical practice within the network, my analytical frameworks served as pivotal instruments for network leadership and the Chancellor, directly empowering and informing high-level healthcare planning and strategic policy formulation.
