# Books and Book Chapters

## Nutritional Science Publication
In 2004, I authored the comprehensive guide titled "Nutrition for Swimmers: A comprehensive nutritional guide for competitive swimmers" (published by the Association of Integrated Council of Noble Swimmers, ICONS, in Bangalore, India). This publication served as a structured, technical resource tailored to competitive athletes, synthesizing complex physiological and nutritional requirements to optimize performance and recovery.

## Community-Based Research Publication
I contributed to the academic literature regarding modern research methodologies and their translation from clinical to community settings. In 2008, I was acknowledged for my subject-matter contributions to the chapter titled "The Internet as platform to do community-based research: A project perspective," which was published in *The Uncharted Path from Clinic-Based to Community-Based Research* (edited by Bonita Stanton, Jennifer Galbraith, and Linda Kaljee; published by NOVA Science Publishers in Hauppauge, NY).
