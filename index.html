I work at the intersection of academic medicine, strategy, and analytics — turning messy, fragmented data into clear narratives, scenario options, and decision tools for deans, department chairs, and health system leaders. My work connects rural health and workforce policy with the realities of GME expansion, LCME/ACGME standards, and financial constraints.

I enjoy designing strategy-ready prototypes — small, opinionated tools that make it easier for leadership to see trade-offs, align stakeholders, and move from discussion to execution. A recurring theme in my work is building governance-minded, future-oriented frameworks for AI adoption, workforce planning, and research growth across schools, hospitals, and health systems.
