# Premium Portfolio — Preservation and Coverage Standard

**Review branch only:** `premium-portfolio-preview-2026-08-21`

## Governing rule

The premium portfolio is being built under a **preservation-first** standard. No existing evidence record is to be deleted, collapsed, or replaced solely because it appears duplicative or because a broader umbrella record exists.

Potential overlaps are treated as one of three types:

1. **True duplicate candidate** — may be flagged for later review, but retained until explicit approval.
2. **Parent/child evidence relationship** — both are retained. The parent explains the broader body of work; the child makes a distinct project, output, audience, role, or decision discoverable.
3. **Related but distinct work** — both are retained as separate evidence records.

No deletions or consolidations should occur without explicit review and approval.

## Source-of-truth inputs

The evidence register must preserve material from all of the following:

- the 97-record live portfolio runtime baseline;
- the complete June 28, 2026 CV;
- the 2014–2019 School of Medicine Strategic Plan Closure Report;
- current portfolio-only tools, dashboards, resources and public scholarship;
- user-confirmed additions supplied during the August 2026 portfolio audit;
- sanitized descriptions of confidential/internal institutional work where public disclosure is appropriate;
- externally verified program context only when specifically researched (for example, Study in India), without converting context into unsupported claims about the user's role.

## Coverage standard

The portfolio is intended to remain useful even when the CV is not opened. Therefore the evidence system should make the following independently discoverable where supported:

- education and certifications;
- career appointments and role progression;
- strategic planning and institutional effectiveness;
- LCME, ACGME, ACCME, SACSCOC and other accreditation/CQI work;
- GME program evaluation, predictive accreditation surveillance and CLER support;
- admissions, UME, Student Affairs and longitudinal learner outcomes;
- GME finance, Medicare/Medicaid policy, contracts, affiliation strategy and health-system partnerships;
- Louisiana commissions, workforce bodies, state-agency consultation and legislative support;
- rural health, HPSA/MUA, AHEC, FQHC, loan-repayment and workforce-pipeline work;
- research enterprise strategy, NIH intelligence, grants and federal reporting;
- Cancer Center/NCI strategy, clinical AI governance and federal AI science analysis;
- AI governance, policy development, educational resources and enterprise technology due diligence;
- faculty affairs, faculty lifecycle analytics, compensation/equity and development evaluation;
- simulation, patient safety, quality improvement, CALS and external simulation collaborations;
- facilities, space, relocation, market and operational planning;
- international academic partnership development, including India Ministry of Education, Study in India opportunity development and IIT Madras;
- funded projects and formal grant roles;
- publications, books/book chapters, posters, oral presentations, governmental technical reports and reviewer service;
- awards, honors, professional service and external swimming/sport-science leadership;
- every current live dashboard, simulator, policy tool, AI guide and public resource that exists in the baseline portfolio.

## Accuracy boundaries

The premium portfolio must not claim:

- direct-report supervision where none existed;
- eight years of staff supervision;
- WSCUC Accreditation Liaison Officer experience;
- BPPE accreditation experience;
- formal SACSCOC liaison/ALO status;
- sole responsibility for LCME, ACGME or ACCME accreditation outcomes;
- authorship of Cancer Center clinical AI pilots that were created by others;
- formal membership in a commission/task force when the source supports only analysis or advisory support;
- implementation/adoption of a recommendation when the source supports only analysis, recommendation or decision support;
- external adoption/use based only on reach or impression metrics.

## Role language

Where supported, use precise distinctions such as:

- `owned the process`;
- `developed`;
- `co-developed`;
- `major contributor`;
- `provided analytical/technical support`;
- `consulted`;
- `served as institutional point of contact`;
- `supported leadership decision-making`;
- `informed`, `enabled`, or `provided decision architecture` when outcome causality is not documented.

## Executive case studies

The Evidence Library is comprehensive. Executive Case Studies are deliberately selective and may synthesize many evidence records. Selection for a case study does **not** determine whether an evidence record is retained.

The public portfolio should remain broadly useful for academic-health-sciences executive roles. Kaiser Permanente Bernard J. Tyson School of Medicine CASO requirements are used as a private optimization/crosswalk layer, not as the public site's identity.
